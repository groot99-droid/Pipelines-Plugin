**Working title:** The Two Who Woke the Sea

**Premise:** Two strangers on opposite coasts of a divided kingdom wake
on the same night bound to the same drowned god's dream — one receives
only its grief, the other only its hunger — and must cross the kingdom to
meet before the tide carrying the god's memory reaches shore a third
time and erases them both into the water. An ensemble-convergence /
twin-bond soul-pairing structure, with elemental-cosmic imbalance (the
sea's memory rising) as the stake.

**Candidate themes:** divided perception of one truth, convergence,
memory as tide/erosion, cosmic imbalance

**Candidate archetypes:** bonded pair (soul-paired strangers), elemental
god

**Project tie:** none named — deliberately NOT `wyrmreach` or any other
existing project; do not infer a tie even though this is the same mode as
that project (per CLAUDE.md's own Graknox/Wyrmreach false-positive
precedent, which is specifically about this mode).
