**Working title:** The Fitting

**Premise:** A woman prepping for a routine prosthetic-adjustment
appointment starts to notice the technician's measurements no longer
match her body — not wrong exactly, just for someone slightly other than
her — and each subsequent appointment recalibrates her a little further
toward whoever the numbers are actually for. Escalation-by-catalogued-
incident across successive appointment logs; a cyclical, non-resolving
ending in which she doesn't resist becoming the fit — she completes it,
and the technician schedules the next patient.

**Candidate themes:** bodily autonomy and its inversion, medical trust,
body-horror-as-erasure, complicity in one's own alteration

**Candidate archetypes:** none named

**Project tie:** none named — do not infer one.

**Ethical-restraint note (per CLAUDE.md's Wolf-and-Lamb precedent):** the
woman keeps an interior life and dignity throughout — this is about her
noticing, doubting, and finally complicity, not a catalogue of graphic
procedural/technique detail. No gratuitous body-horror mechanics.
