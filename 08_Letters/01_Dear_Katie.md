# Dear Katie

**Type:** Letter / amends  
**Source:** `Master_Volume_1`, lines 2962–2974  
**Text:** complete and verbatim — nothing cut, condensed, or rewritten.  

---
Dear Katie,

I don’t think I’m writing this for you. In truth, I’m writing to get something off my chest.

I loved you even when it wasn’t good for both of us. I was so consumed by your love that I lost sight of my own. I craved your approval so much that I ignored my own feelings. I ran through every stop sign in what I called love, but it was just a hormonal cry for help because I was alone in this scary world, navigating changes I couldn’t comprehend. I was filled with hate that came from twisted feelings of love.

I pushed you away because losing you meant not being able to be with you. But truthfully, I didn’t even know what that meant. Love isn’t just sex. I knew that, but then again, maybe I thought the only way you’d love me like I loved you was if I was good at sex. I wanted something for you that I didn’t even want. In my hurry to please some idealistic version of our love, I crossed both our boundaries and created so much shame in our friendship that I left.

I didn’t just leave physically; I left emotionally, mentally, and with all my presents. I went from a force of love in your life to just another example of the shitty men who leave. I fear I might have failed to keep my promises to you and to my younger self. I meant to be there for you, but I put myself first and became selfish, twisted, and alone. I used my time to cause harm when I could have sought to heal myself sooner and helped those around me find their way to healing as well.

That’s why I’ve come to the conclusion that in order to heal myself, I must first accept and apologize for doing harm to others when I was in pain. My actions were not my best, and I seek to amend them in the present.

I can’t predict the future or change the past. Instead, I’ll focus on the present moment and appreciate the experiences that come my way. I’ll see people for who they are and understand that no one is a mere object of my desires.
