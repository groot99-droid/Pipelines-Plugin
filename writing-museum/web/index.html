The Writing Museum

Creative Writing

A hall with a door to every work. Behind each door, one room shaped by that work: its passages hang on the walls as panels, verbatim, and the room's look follows the work's own mode, moods and motifs.

Every panel is the author's own text, cited to its vault path and lines. Each room was made through the studio pipeline and has a note that says how.
