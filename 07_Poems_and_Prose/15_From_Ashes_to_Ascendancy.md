 # From Ashes to Ascendancy

### I. The Seeds of Burden  
At eight, a weight of sorrow pressed on tender frame,  
The world dismissed my worth and cloaked me in its shame.  
A teacher’s scorn declared that I was trouble in a guise,  
Yet lonely halls became my strength, where silent hope would rise.

They threatened, “Stop your tears or face a harsher fate,”    
But every sequestered tear forged courage over hate.  
With honors bright as beacons and diplomas to defy  
The twisted words they threw at me, I soared from every lie.

---

### II. The Tower of Silence  
Before me loomed the giants—monsters cloaked in human guise,  
Whose vile, forbidden acts stole trust and dimmed the skies.  
In that crushing, fateful moment, innocence was torn apart,  
Transforming dreams of gentle grace into chains upon my heart.

Their breach of sacred boundaries etched a scar I can’t ignore,  
Yet even in that darkest hour, from pain I learned to soar.  
Though shadows linger long in echoes of the acts they wove,  
I lit a flame of inner strength no terror can remove.

---

### III. The Stolen Narrative  
They penned a tale in twisted ink, a story not my own,  
Distorting truth with crafted lies until my voice was overthrown.  
Voices rose to mute my plea, to cage the truth inside,  
Yet from the silence of that theft, a rebel soul replied.

I reclaimed each stolen line and reshaped every wounded part,  
Transforming manufactured history into a testament of heart.  
No puppet strings of false accounts can bind what is set free—  
I own my truth, my scars, and now I write my destiny.

---

### IV. The Shield of the Moment  
In storms of doubt and bitter trials, I stand a fortress strong,  
Holding back the ghosts of past, rejecting every wrong.  
With every steady heartbeat, I summon fierce and steady power,  
Defying threats and bitter voices with a spirit that won’t cower.

I arm myself with memories turned into badges of my fight,  
Each step a rhythmic anthem carved in defiant, pulsing light.  
The silence after all their venom bursts with victories untold—  
In reclaiming all I once had lost, I transmute the dark to gold.

---

### V. Epilogue – Rising in Resilience  
Those hollow words that bound with “I love you, but” like chains,  
Could never root in a resolve that triumphs over pains.  
The blaze of hate and anguish that scorched my tender days  
Now fuels a vibrant courage, igniting ever-brighter rays.

Today, my eyes hold embers that no threat can disbelieve,  
For I rise each day unbroken—a phoenix born to weave  
A life of raw resistance, a story fiercely self-made.  
A survivor’s truth affirmed in every step and every shade.
