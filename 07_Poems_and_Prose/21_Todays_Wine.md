# Todays Wine

**Type:** Poem  
**Source:** `Master_Volume_2`, lines 91–108  
**Text:** complete and verbatim — nothing cut, condensed, or rewritten.  

---
Todays Wine

Today's wine is particularly fine,  
Exquisitely crafted, a true divine.  

For yesterday's wine has slipped from our grasp,  
Its taste lingers sweet, but it doth not last

And tomorrow's wine has yet to delight,  
For it’s future’s promise, not here in our sight.  

Today’s wine is ours, to savor and share,  
Though it may not be perfect, it’s still worth a care.  
Remember, not all things are destined to last,  
For memories linger, but they are the past.

Embrace the present, let your spirit flow,  
For today is a gift; in this moment, we grow.
