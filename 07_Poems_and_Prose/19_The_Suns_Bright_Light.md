# The Sun's Bright Light

**Type:** Poem  
**Source:** `Master_Volume_2`, lines 74–83  
**Text:** complete and verbatim — nothing cut, condensed, or rewritten.  
**Note:** Untitled in source; title taken from the first line.  

---
The sun's bright light, a golden hue,  
Forever young, yet ancient too.  
Its beams stretch wide from corner to ledge,  
With my eye I see the sky, where azure sings and pledges.  
As it draws near, its glow is clear,  
A gentle warmth that I hold dear.  
I recall what I've always known—  
Like a tree in December, its light has shone.  
For it is life, and it gives us breath,  
A radiant gift that conquers death.
