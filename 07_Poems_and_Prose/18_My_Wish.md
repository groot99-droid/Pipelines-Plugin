# My Wish

**Type:** Poem  
**Source:** `Master_Volume_2`, lines 44–64  
**Text:** complete and verbatim — nothing cut, condensed, or rewritten.  

---
My wish

My wish is not for material worries.  
I do not beg for gold.  
I do not wish to never grow old.  
My wish is not wealth like winter flurries.

My wish is not for love never-ending.  
I do not groan for fable.  
I do not grovel for a seat at the table.  
My wish is not for a will unbending.

No my wish is to be me.  
Not better or worse.  
Not to live with remorse.  
My wish is to be.

My wish is to be me.  
No matter who it angers.  
No matter the strangers.  
My wish is to be, who ever the fuck that my be.
