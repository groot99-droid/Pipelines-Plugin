Building dreams from the top down

Michelin Star Chef - I want tons of successful restaurants and Michelin Stars. I want to train with some of the greatest chefs in the most renowned European, South/Central American, African, and Asian restaurants from around the world. I want to create food that has never existed in ways that were never thought of. I want to bring an alien/new flair to food that turns the world upside down. 

Renowned Artist - I want my art to leave an impression on future artists. I want reach like Van Gogh, with the influence of Picasso, and the beauty of Rembrandt.

Singer - I want to be a singer. I don’t care if I’m the best or even if I make it big I just want a few songs that people can relate to and gives a whole new meaning to the world I have felt something all along
