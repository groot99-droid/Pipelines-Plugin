The Bloody Alarm Clock: A Horror Story About a Timepiece That Won’t Let You Wake

Some cursed objects announce themselves.  
This one just… ticks.

The Bloody Alarm Clock follows an ordinary person who inherits an old bedside clock with a strange quirk: it never rings out loud, yet every night it plays inside their head. The sound isn’t a tone or a melody — it’s a pressure, a pulse, a presence that drags them into vivid, terrifying dreams.

At first, the dreams are just unsettling.  
Then they become unbearable.

Each night brings a new nightmare: running out of time, suffering mysterious internal pain, drowning, collapsing, or meeting some unseen fate. And every morning, the victim wakes with real‑world echoes of what they dreamed:

• Waking soaked after dreaming of drowning  
• Feeling faint after dreaming of losing blood  
• Hearing phantom ticking long after opening their eyes


Worst of all, their ears begin to bleed when they wake — a quiet, chilling sign that the clock is doing something far more dangerous than disturbing their sleep.

The victim tries everything to get rid of it.  
Throwing it away.  
Smashing it.  
Leaving it behind.

But the clock always returns — sometimes on the nightstand, sometimes in a dream, sometimes in a place they don’t remember putting it. It’s as if the clock is choosing them, not the other way around.

As sleep becomes a threat, the victim starts avoiding it entirely. Exhaustion leads to waking hallucinations — daydreams that feel just as real and terrifying as the nightmares. The line between dream and reality dissolves, and the ticking becomes constant, inescapable.

Eventually, the victim realizes the truth:

The clock doesn’t measure time.  
It consumes it.

And when it finally takes the last of theirs, the clock quietly moves on — appearing in the home of its next unsuspecting owner, ready to begin the cycle again.

The Bloody Alarm Clock is a psychological horror story about dread, inevitability, and the terrifying idea that something as harmless as a bedside clock could be counting down to your end.
