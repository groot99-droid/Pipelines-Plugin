The Show must go on

Starts off with a completely normal kid who one day hits his head. He wakes in the hospital surrounded by his loved ones but something is different he just can’t place it. Soon he gets home and that’s when weird stuff starts happening he notices at first but before long it just becomes normal for him. The problem he contracted is that his life takes on the appearance of a sitcom. He pops in and out from reality. He believes his friends are cross over characters constantly referring to them by their last names when he meets them as if it’s a tv show. He bursts out in laughter over jokes that a talk show host tells him while nobody else knows what’s funny. Work in progress…
