Project: Man Eater


Deep in the woods and mountains of North Dakota, the CIA constructed the "Project Man Eater." Fisk, a newly recruited member of the CIA, has been deployed and drafted as a member of "Project Man Eater." When he arrives on base, something seems amiss; the facility is heavily armored but not to keep things out, but instead to keep them in. Even though the barracks are relatively new, they appear rundown; deterioration and other issues have already occurred. Although he would like to say this is the weirdest thing here, it is not; the crew members look sick and slightly anorexic. There are giant claw marks on the walls, and for some reason, it is freezing. Sure, outside, it is winter, and snow is covering the ground, but even inside, you can still see your breath; it almost seemed colder inside than out. These things had unsettled Fisk, but he had been in worse places. His first time drafted, he had been stationed in New Mexico, where the Barracks got so hot many recruits died of heat stroke, and worst of all was the smell. When night came, everyone's socks and other articles of clothing, drenched in sweat the day before, would fry in the night and smell like a cloud of body odor suffocating the whole base. Many crew members developed lung issues; Fisk had been one of the lucky ones. A barked order brought him out of his daze.  
"Sir yes Sir," he called back. "Agent Fisk, let me show you to your quarters. I am the head of this project, and we won't go by all that formal fluff. You can just call me Sam," Sam summoned from across the room. Fisk quickly followed him down many straight and narrow hallways, each about 4 feet wide but a whopping 15 feet high. They stop at a door just as disproportionate as the hallways. "This is where you'll be staying, so get comfortable. There are a few rules I must provide you with first, however. Rule one: You will be eating a strict diet, and that means no food outside what you get in the cafeteria. Rule two: Do not, under any circumstances, leave this room after 10:00 precisely. Finally, rule three, you are not to leave the base under any circumstance, no matter the reason." Sam stressed the last part and left it to hang like a doomsday cloud, covering the barracks in a smoke screen of fear. "Sir yes Sir," Fisk replied flatly. Sam left without another word, and down the hallway, he went.  
Later that night, Fisk was in bed curled up in 3 sleeping bags, still shivering, when he heard a noise like a call. Low melodic groaning, almost flute-like, caught his ear. He felt a mix of emotions run over him. He felt scared but also slightly comforted. It was an odd assortment of feelings. He looked at the clock, and it was precisely 10:00. He wondered what it could be when his bunkmate got up and opened the door. Fisk noticed that his bunkmate might have been the skinniest man he had ever witnessed. In fact, he thought almost for a second he could see his bones. Fisk watched as his bunkmate left the room and closed the door behind him. Suddenly, the sound of groaning stopped, and he was alone. Fisk didn't even try to stop his bunkmate. It was as if the sound had called him, and they had both excepted. He suddenly felt drained, and he fell to his pillow.  
He woke to the sun shining in through the window. It was still cold, and his bunkmate hadn't returned, but he couldn't quite make himself feel sad about it for some reason. It reminded him of the feeling he felt last night when he had just accepted him leaving. Fisk put on his uniform and went to the cafeteria, where he was served a disturbing meal. It looked like lab-grown meat but not the usual garbage served in most CIA facilities. This was different, almost too disturbing to eat, but he did, and it was like nothing he had ever eaten before. He didn't enjoy the taste, but he wanted more. It was like he had become more hungry by eating it, and soon, he had eaten his entire daily ration when he was told he couldn't have anymore. They gave him a loaf of bread, which made him feel full, and he continued his day. 
He felt lighter for some reason, almost like he had grown thinner since lunch. He felt around his waist; he had become slightly leaner. He could now feel his rib cage prominently push against his skin. His knuckles began to grow whiter and lighter, too. It was odd but not uncommon; he had heard stories of agents drafted from one extreme climate to another and that their bodies had reacted in very different ways. So he paid it no mind and continued down the hall towards his bunk. He walked through the insanely disproportionate door to his bunk and saw Sam waiting inside. "Good afternoon, agent; I just wanted to inform you that your former bunkmate has been transferred, and he will no longer be stationed here. We had the conversation last night around 10:00, so about when you would have been asleep, and he said he didn't feel like this was the right project for him. We deployed him somewhere else and have decided to provide you with a new bunkmate who will be here sometime this evening." Sam provided all this information while, in the back of Fisk's mind, he knew something was off.  
Fisk lay in bed. He looked at the clock. The hands touched 10 again, and he heard the same groaning as before. This time, he felt like it was calling him. He got out of bed and slunk to the door. His new bunkmate, Jax, got up with him. A silent agreement was voiced between them; they both wanted to know what it was and why they had been lied to. Fisk had clued Jax in on what had been happening: how Sam had lied to him about his last bunkmate and that something horrible must have happened for him to completely go missing; how he had felt something off about the shape of this place and everything else that had been going through his head. Jax was just as eager to uncover the secrets as Fisk was, and they were both about to come to the point of no return. They had followed the groaning down the hallway and could feel themselves getting closer. They could now tell it was indeed a flute they heard, and the air felt frozen. Fisk's blood began to get cold, and his fingers showed the first sign of frostbite. Fisk could feel his mind leaving him as the cold stabbed at his brain. Still, he pushed through. They rounded the corner, and what they saw shattered their puny minds, scattering what was left of their sanity.  
There stood a man pale as snow, bones bare, and skin stretched thin; he stood at an enormous height, almost touching the ceiling, with his anorexic limbs seemingly stout. He played the flute slowly through heaved breaths. The thing looked upon the two trespassers who dared to linger and uttered, "Beware the Windigo, for I am he. Follow in my footsteps, and you will become me." With a gust of cold mist, the Windigo vanished into thin air. Fisk could feel his skin tightening around his bones, like his body was eating itself from the inside out. He felt sick, but simultaneously, a growing strength in him. Two dazed by what they had just witnessed, neither Jax nor Fisk heard the footsteps that slowly and silently approached behind them. Suddenly, cloths wrapped around their mouths, and a gas leaked into their lungs, causing them to lose consciousness. They were dragged down many halls, stairs, and long dark corridors until finally they stopped. Fisk's eyes shot open; the drugs had worn off, and he knew what had happened. Sam slipped out of the shadows, a smirk painted across his face, his eyes twisted in an attempt to mask his dissatisfaction. Fisk struggled, but he had been tied to a nearby stalagmite that jutted up from the cave floor.  
"Where am I, and what are you really up to here?" Fisk roared with all the strength he could muster. The drugs he had been given seemed to do much more than just put him to sleep. " Do you not realize what this place is? Long ago, back in a far less hospitable time, Native Americans would suffer through long and harsh winters where, in the most devastating of times, they had to resort to cannibalism; this brought about the birth of the Windigo. A curse was placed upon those who had partaken of the flesh of another human? My superiors wish to harvest this curse and use it for scientific advancements, but first, we must have a surplus of test subjects." Sam continued, but Fisk interrupted. "You still haven't answered me; what is this place? Why do you want us? We are not Windigos, nor will a single man in his right mind eat another human." Sam's face twisted and quickly began again. "You must be kidding me. I didn't expect you to be so stupid. This place is the first recorded birthplace of a Windigo. Here, we have begun our project by feeding you all flesh and other chemicals to cause the same effect but at a quickened rate. Look at you; you're skin is tightening around your bones, and you are slowly becoming one yourself. Soon, you will be our next test subject, but we won't make the same mistakes as we have with the rest." Fisk's eyes were tearing up, and a lump formed in his throat. He couldn't speak he couldn't think. He felt another cloth go around his mouth, and once again, he was filled with the toxic fumes. The room went black.  
When he woke up, what he saw horrified him. He had now been entirely transformed. A tube ran through his arm. Parts of his body, which had been amputated, were being examined on a nearby table. He was now a test subject and would never see the light of day again, but that wasn't all he knew. Deep down, this would only spread. As his mind went blank and his thoughts became those of an animal, he knew he would always hate these people but would never get revenge. He was like an animal in a zoo... The end.
