Low In The Water

The boat’s motor hummed low and rhythmic as it cut through the water. Shadows cast from the boughs low beams loomed like demons into the marshy bed. The mud squelched underneath as the boat shot through  The Waffle House sign flickered: bright yellow, then dark, yellow, then dark, yellow… then dark. Water dripped from the roof as the weather worsened. Lightning struck in the distance, and thunder sounded like an explosive drum. Ding ding, the door chimed as it shut fiercely behind a hooded silhouette. Then suddenly… the man pulled back his hoodie to reveal a kind, sweet-looking face of an old man. His grey mustache curled from the humid weather but with little to no hair atop his sweat-beaded head.

Goede dag, the man said in a high-pitched, squeaky German accent. "I'm here for your finest waffles," he said with a squirm of delight and plopped himself at one of the tables, kicking his legs up onto the seat across from him.
