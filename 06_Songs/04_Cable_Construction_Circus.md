# Cable Construction Circus

**Type:** Song / work-site narrative rap  
**Source:** `Master_Volume_1`, lines 2974–3075  
**Text:** complete and verbatim — nothing cut, condensed, or rewritten.

Seven in the mornin’, sun barely risen,  
Locates all wrong like somebody wasn’t listenin’,  
Half the crew standin’ around like they lost in a vision,  
Boss pulled up and immediately started the bitchin’

——

Step right up, hard hat on, leave your pride at the gate,  
Best damn circus in the county, don’t you dare be late,  
Cable construction circus, everybody losin’ they mind,  
Clowns on the trencher, monkey one step behind,

Roll up — big top sunk deep in the mud,  
Cable construction circus, runnin’ on coffee and blood

Paperwork from yesterday, locates by tonight —

Machine hit rock at nine, gas line right at noon,  
Should’ve known from the jump we’d be cryin’ pretty soon —

Now Greg — Lord Greg — rolled up with the strangest energy,  
Lawn chair in the shade like the job site’s his amenity,  
BlueChew in his pocket like it’s doing him a favor,  
Useless as the man, pill ain’t finding no savior,  
Looked at the driller’s legs, said it slow like it was normal —  
“Them legs gave me a hard on” — nothing about it formal,

Barkin’ out instructions he ain’t qualified to give,  
Soft-dick bastard telling hardworking men how to live,  
More excuses than the pill got listed on the label —  
Greg, sit your ass down and get up from the table


(drops to almost a whisper, deliberate)  
Then there’s Ethan —  
Only reason this whole circus ain’t burned to the ground,  
Fixin’ everybody’s disasters without makin’ a sound,  
You can see it in his eyes if you look long enough —  
Tired, worn, runnin’ thin, done with all of this stuff,  
Cleanin’ up behind Rob, cleanin’ up behind Greg,  
Jacob drillin’ crooked with his phone up to his head,  
Ethan’s the only sumbitch here who genuinely knows the trade,
