# Arrivals

**THORNE & EXPANSE LINE**
**HOLLOWMERE SPUR**
**ARRIVALS AND DEPARTURES**

*To be kept at the platform by a servant of the Company who can write. No Sunday service.*

Kept by: *Clem. Platform crew.*

---

## Head note

This is a new book, because the timetable is new. It is in force from 2 October 1893. The old book, if there was one, went down the Line, or nobody handed it to me. This one begins on the 2nd. Whoever went before the 2nd went in some other book, and that includes Father Bray. I am writing down that I noticed.

I am on this book because I am the one on the boards who can fill in a form. I am also the one who reads it back to them. They let me, because it passes the cold. Len says a thing read out weighs less. He has never lifted a word.

This book counts who comes off at Day and who gets on at Dusk.

It does not count these:

- **Freight.** Freight is the Conductor's manifest. Rule 2: "The Company counts the crate, not the contents."
- **Car 3.** Car 3 is the Undertaker's. When a box goes out I write that it went. The count is his.
- **Persons of rank.** Rule 6: "Persons of rank and their escorts travel in the private car and are not entered on the passenger count." So when the private car comes, I count the dogs. The form has no rule against dogs.
- **Anyone walking the ties.** They do not come off the train, so they are not in this book.

The law of this book is Rule 9. I read it aloud the first morning to Len and Ernie and the frost: "A passenger booked in is a passenger counted out."

Counted out where, Ernie asked. At the Junction. I have never seen the Junction send a count back.

I came up the Line once myself, smaller than Ernie. I don't remember the train. That is the one arrival this book will not have.

> *In the bag, wrapped in a Gazette, was a child's pair of boots, new, the laces tied together through the eyes.*
> *He's not in my Off column. He got off at the wrong stop.*
