# Codex of the End of the Line

*Coldwater Territory, 1893.*

> HOLLOWMERE — EST. BY THE RAILROAD EXPANSE

---

## Cosmic Origin

What follows is what the Territory believes. It is not what the Territory knows.

**The Line — "the Iron"**
Tie by wooden tie, spike by iron spike. It brings flour, whiskey, the mail and, when the town riots, the Baron. At Dusk it takes back whoever it came for, and the coffins ride in the back car.
*Believed in: HollowMere, and by every debtor at BlackPine on payroll day.*

**The Mere — "the Drowned"**
Fen, where the lake was let down into Vane's tin gallery to lay the Line's last mile. It breathes what the town calls mere-rot and the Company calls miasma. It keeps a tide with no sea, and someone still circles the low water on an old chart.
*Believed in: HollowMere.*

**The Pines — "the Black"**
The whole forest. Gallows Wood is its near edge, where the town hangs people; the Fenwood is its drowned heart, with the hanging oak, the charcoal clamp, and a drowned lantern that has not gone out. Its creed is short: the pines were not empty.
*Believed in: HollowMere and BlackPine.*

**The Sea — "Beyond the Reef"**
The island, the fog, the Mother of Mothers. Inland it is known by three things only: rumour, the wake-loaf, and a dead man's name stencilled on crates.
*Believed in: Port-au-Levain. Inland, half-believed.*

Not a god. Not a law. A timetable.

---

## Central Premise

1893, the year of the Panic. The railroads are failing, the banks are failing, the strikes are spreading. Coldwater Territory is not yet a state. The law is thin, so the Company is the law. Everywhere else is "back east". Three towns stand at the end of the line, and each keeps its own clock. One dead man's name is on all three.

"Seven days' walk from the nearest soul. Hemmed in by black pines, hollow caves, and freezing mud." Nobody comes to the end of the line "unless they're running from something—or hunting for it."

"In HollowMere, nobody kills for nothing. Everyone has a price, a prayer, or a vendetta."

## The Three Settlements

**HOLLOWMERE — the Thorne & Expanse Line**
Owner: the Railroad Barons; above them Cornelius Thorne, alive, who comes by train.
Clock: the Daily Train. It arrives at the start of each Day and leaves at Dusk.
Currency & Tax: Company Scrip, collected as the Clerk's Midday Rent. Unpaid by curfew means debtors' prison or forced labour at the mill.
Identity prop: the role card. The card in your hand at dawn is who you are.
Motto: HOLLOWMERE — EST. BY THE RAILROAD EXPANSE.
Law: Detective → Police Officer → Judge.
Places: the square and the Town Bell; the pump and the notice board; the Drowned Lantern; Mallory Apothecary; Widow Vane's parlour, the portrait turned to the wall; Crumb's Ovens and its ten plates; Gallows Wood; the Fenwood; the Tin Gallery and the Flooded Gallery; Vane Manor, its clock stopped at 11:47; Hale Barn, a wolf pelt on the door; St Brannock's and its Company bell; the Undercroft.
Dread: don't miss the train.

**BLACKPINE — the Harrow Timber & Coal Syndicate**
Owner: the Syndicate, which bought the camp when the tin ran out.
Clock: the Payroll Train, the Shift-Whistle, the Midnight Roll Call.
Currency & Tax: Syndicate scrip, against the Head Tallyman's quota. A missed quota brings a fine and a ration cut.
Identity prop: the branded tally log on the Tally Rack. Ten billets of oak, fir and pine, slotted by density, each branded with a job, a debt and a sin. The log on your rack at dawn is who you are.
Motto: PRODUCTION IS OBLIGATION.
Law: Camp Investigator → Mine Warden → Circuit Judge.
Places: the yard, the broken trestle, the flume; the Bunkhouse; Vane Sawmill; the kilns; the Workings, one name chalked out on the tally board; the Company Office and the Harrow line, which rarely works; the Forge; the Deep Level; Widow-Maker Ridge; the Pit-Head; the Infirmary.
Dread: feeding the furnace, or fuel.
Later, typed in a margin: GROUNDS 99, 1952. The same motto over the same gate. The rack and the whistle stand. A program called ANOMALY UNKNOWN keeps the books.

**PORT-AU-LEVAIN — the Sovereign Sourdough Council**
Owner: the Council, under the Master Bakers' lineage.
Clock: the Morning Ferry.
Currency & Tax: Guild Copper and yeast tokens, paid as the platter tariff at the proofing bell. Unpaid means the loaf is Unregistered.
Identity prop: the Wake Loaf on its silver platter in the Hall of Platters. The loaf on your plate at dawn is who you are.
Motto: Bread is law, lineage and life.
Law: Inquisitor → Guild Bailiff → High Chancellor.
Places: the Great Hearth; the Windmill Cliffs; the Subterranean Yeast Vault; the Salt-Flats and Smuggler's Wharf; the Apothecary; Gallows Square; the lighthouse; the Counting-House; the Port Tavern.
Dread: when the ferry casts off and the fog swallows the lamps, "the fermentation turns sour."
Earlier, typed in a margin: 1787, LETTERS PATENT OF THE CROWN. THE HEARTH FOR THE POOR TO 1789. THE TURN. 1793, THE COMMONS IN THE HALL AND A BLADE OFF THE FERRY.

Train. Ferry. Whistle.

## The Four Currencies

**Faith — buys sanctuary**
Earned by attending chapel and by confessing. Spent on protection against conversion and on clearing your name of small rumours. Its coin is Indulgences and Parish Seals.
*Run out, and the bell finds you with nowhere to kneel.*

**Scrip — buys tomorrow**
Earned from the train trade, the ovens, the bank and the heist. Spent on tools, rent, quota and fines. Its coin is Company scrip, Syndicate scrip and Guild Copper.
*Run out, and it ends at the mill, the quota or the Unregistered loaf.*

**Rumour — buys a vote**
Earned by steaming open mail, by pigeon cylinders and by shift slips. Spent at the trial table. Its coin is the Dirty Deed Ledger Slip.
*It never runs out. It hardens.*

**Status — buys a station**
Earned by putting scrip, flour, timber and coal into one's own station, three tiers deep. Spent on what the station unlocks.
*Run out, and you are only your prop.*

**The Deflection Loop**
- A secret letter, held to the steam, becomes a blackmail notice.
- The notice goes to the back-alley market, and comes back as scrip.
- The notice goes to the jury, and comes back as a vote.
- Scrip laid on the altar comes back as an Indulgence.
- A note slipped into a loaf comes back as an innocent customer.

A secret, a coin, a prayer and a blackmail notice all spend the same. Deflect it before the knock.

## The Four Vendettas: the Four Ways a Town Breaks

**CONVERSION — faith breaks**
HollowMere: the Zealot against the Vicar. The Zealot kills him and Skin-Walks in his robes for as long as the body goes unreported. Converts become the Dark Positions: Minion, Mob Boss, Cultist, High Priest. Ash-and-blood sigils on five buildings. Six followers at the Deep Cave Altar or the desecrated chapel, and then "the sky turns crimson, the town bell tolls backward". / Port-au-Levain: Leaven-Rot against the Abbot of the Crust. / BlackPine: the Timber-Wolf against the Forest Chaplain, with its pack and the Blood-Moon Howl.
Sign left behind: candlewax.

The Vicar's last words:

> "In the end I sought nothing else but to serve God. And serve him I did. With this death's last breath I pray this prayer: may the Almighty grant me the strength of ten in the next life. And in return, tenfold my own."

His memorial is a lavender field, a lone orange tree and a blue sky. He returns as the Ascended Vicar: an iron crosier, a pillar of light, an aura tenfold.
*Tag: the town converts, or the town is converted.*

**SABOTAGE — the Company breaks**
HollowMere: the Activist against the Baron. Fire in the Mill. Dynamite on the Tracks. The Bank Heist. A soapbox at the bell; the Baron by train; then the Redemption vote. If she survives it, she becomes plain Townsfolk again. / Port-au-Levain: the Pyre-Master against the Grand Inspector-General. / BlackPine: the Slate-Breaker against the Syndicate Inspector.
Sign left behind: black powder.
*Tag: the grip loosens, and the town has to decide whether to forgive the hand that loosened it.*

**HEIST — the till breaks**
HollowMere: the Bandit, with a bandana, a six-shooter and four scores: the bakery, where he leaves a gold-coin tip; the chapel plate; the bank vault; the express safe. / Port-au-Levain: the Smuggler-Blackguard, with a pocket-mortar and swapped loaves. / BlackPine: the Company Enforcer, with a Winchester, swapped logs and the Sawmill Blade.
Sign left behind: a brass casing (.45), and flour dust.
*Tag: everything is owned, so everything can be taken.*

**CONTRACT — a person breaks**
HollowMere: the Hitman. A black-wax envelope comes in the mailbag: "Burn the Letter & Keep Your Mundane Life, OR Accept the Baron's Contract." A needle or a derringer; he leaves on the train. "Take only its mark." / Port-au-Levain: the Black-Crust Contract, with a pigeon cylinder and a nightshade glaze. / BlackPine: the Pit-Poisoner, and a slip in a lunchbox. By 1952 the slip is an enlistment, its department under a black bar, and the hand that takes it up is the Secret Agent's.
Sign left behind: a wax seal.
*Tag: nobody starts as the Hitman.*

## The Three Ladders of Law

- HollowMere: Detective → Police Officer → Judge. Verdicts: Acquittal / a Heavy Fine / Death, by the gallows trapdoor.
- Port-au-Levain: Inquisitor → Guild Bailiff → High Chancellor. Verdicts: Acquittal / a Guild Fine / Trial by Fire in the Great Hearth.
- BlackPine: Camp Investigator → Mine Warden → Circuit Judge. Verdicts: Acquittal / Scrip Forfeiture / the Great Sawmill Blade.

Three ladders. Three rungs. Three verdicts. One of them is always rope, fire or blade.

*The town has spoken.*

## The Rest of the Town

**Detective — "the magnifier"**
Carries a case file and a stamp. Works the clues by daylight. Knows what the clues say, not what they mean.
*"Collect, inspect, cross-reference."*

**Doctor — "Doc"**
Patches the living and opens the dead. Stands wherever the bell rings. Knows when someone died, and that is what breaks alibis.
*"Keep people alive and read the dead."*

**Vicar — "the new one"**
Came on the train after the old priest had gone. Stands at St Brannock's, under the Company bell. Knows everything, sooner or later.
*"Everyone in Hollowmere confesses to you sooner or later."*

**Watchman — "the lantern on two legs"**
Walks the lanes after dark. Stands at one chosen door each night. Knows who came to that door, and nothing about the others.
*"You walk the lanes after dark. Each night, choose one door to stand by."*

**Herbalist — "the bottle woman"**
Grows and brews. Keeps two bottles. Knows the difference between them.
*"One bottle saves, one bottle ends."*

**Gamekeeper — "Vane's man"**
Was paid by a dead man to keep a wood. Stands in Gallows Wood. Knows the den.
*"Vane paid you to keep the wood. If they come for you, you will not go alone."*

**Butler — "the keys"**
Serves a house whose master is dead. Stands in the manor hall. Knows every door.
*"You know every door in Hollowmere."*

**Child — "the small one"**
Plays hopscotch under a lantern. Hides where grown men do not look. Remembers.
*"You are small and quiet. Hide, watch, remember."*

**Lamplighter — "the light"**
Lights the square. Stands between the lanterns and the pines. Knows what the wolves are afraid of.
*"You light the lanterns that keep the wolves off the square."*

**Baker — "Crumb's"**
Bakes for the living and the dead. Stands at the ovens before anyone wakes. Knows who bought which loaf.
*"Up before anyone, flour to the elbows."*

**Fisher — "the channel man"**
Has no sea to fish, so works the mere channels. Knows the tide that should not be there.
*"The fog keeps the boats in."*

**Tinner — "the shut hand"**
Worked the tin until the tin ran out. Stands at the mouth of the gallery. Knows the flooded workings, and the name of the first man they kept.
*"The mine is shut; your hands are not."*

**Drifter — "the stranger"**
Came in on the train with no business here. Stands at the gallows. Wants to be chosen for it.
*"You want to hang."*

**Town superstitions**
They say there is always one who walks in a borrowed character, strikes after dark when the room is empty, washes at the well, and then is the loudest voice asking who did it.
They say there is always a second, who never holds the knife, only the alibi.
They say the knife is inherited, and that the Heir is the one who picks it up.
Nobody has proved it. Nobody has stopped saying it.

They say the town is waiting for others: a Ranger with a crossbow, a Thief who leaves by sea, a Medium with seance chalk.

**Named townsfolk.** Mrs Grieves, at the tavern. Old Fennick, at the old lake-light. Doc Mallory. Tuck, the ferry boy, who poles the marsh. Tamsin Crumb, of the island. Harbourmaster Pell, at the marsh dock. Mother Moll, at her charcoal hut in the Fenwood. Foreman Hask, on the BlackPine yard. Ada Kerrow, at the forge. The Collier, at the kilns. Widow Vane, in the parlour.

## Bestiary

**The Mere and the Fenwood**

**Mere-wolves — the Mere**
Also called the Black Pine Wolves. They hunt the edge of the light and never cross it. Mere-rot makes them bold.
*Dread: the lantern going out.*

**Bog-wisps — the Mere**
Lights low over the fen that are not lanterns. They lead walkers off the path and in.
*Dread: following.*

**The Beast — the Tin Gallery**
Something asleep in the cave. The mere-rot wakes it. Nothing more is written here.
*Dread: the cave.*

**Mere-rot — everywhere the fen breathes**
The town knows it by its symptoms:
- the wolves grow bold;
- footsteps sound where nobody walks;
- the cough comes.
*Dread: the fog in everyone's chest.*

**Field and town**
Boar, at the wood's edge. Bats, in the galleries. Rats, in the Undercroft. Crows, on the gallows. Gulls, following the ferry.

**The Sea**

**Tide-Ghouls — the Salt-Flats**
They come in with the water. They stay when the water goes.
*Dread: low tide.*

**Salt-Flat Drowners — the Salt-Flats**
They lie under the flats when the tide is out.
*Dread: the shortcut.*

**The Pines and the Workings**

**Timber-Wolves — the Black Pines**
They take the ridge and the trails.
*Dread: the whistle you did not answer.*

**Tunnel Crawlers — the Workings**
They live below the last lamp.
*Dread: the dark level.*

The pack of the Timber-Wolf vendetta (Timber-Stalker, Pit-Fiend, Den-Keeper, Alpha-Born) is not listed here. See Conversion.

## Factions and Enforcers

**The Thorne & Expanse Line — HollowMere**
It wants the line paid for. It enforces through the Clerk, the rent and the train. It fears the soapbox at the bell.
*Colours: the station sign.*

**The Pinkertons — the train**
They come when the town riots, with the Baron and the guard dogs. They leave when he does.
*Colours: the dogs.*

**The Harrow Timber & Coal Syndicate — BlackPine**
It wants the quota met. It enforces through the Tallyman, the Roll Call and the fine. It fears an empty bunk at midnight.
*Colours: PRODUCTION IS OBLIGATION.*

**The Program — Grounds 99, 1952**
It wants ████. It enforces through the roll, the rack and the dawn tribunal, and through the four services that each say their interest is limited. It fears nothing on paper.
*Colours: a black bar.*

**The Order Enforcement Agents (OEAs) — BlackPine**
They are sent out when the Roll Call finds empty bunks or a steam main ruptures. Gas masks. Searchlights.
*Colours: the searchlight.*

**The Sovereign Sourdough Council — Port-au-Levain**
It wants every loaf registered. It enforces through the tariff, the Counting-House and the Hearth. It fears the sour turn.
*Colours: the guild seal.*

**The four of the Hall — Port-au-Levain, 1787–1793**
The Gallery wants rank and the Patent; the Guild wants whoever pays; the Commons want bread, then the Hall; the Abbey wants the Mother pure and the keys kept. They enforce through the Hearth, the scale, the Square and the sacrament, in that order, and then in the other order.
*Colours: gold leaf, flour-white, a red crust, burlap.*

**The Iron-Wardens — Port-au-Levain**
The Council's hand. They come when the yeast-tax goes unpaid across the island, or when the Grand Inspector is threatened. Brass-plated leather and shock-staves; they haul suspects to Gallows Square.
*Colours: the shock-staff.*

**The cult of the Dark Mass — HollowMere**
It wants six. It enforces by conversion. It fears the body being reported.
*Colours: ash and blood on five buildings.*

**The union — the saloon**
It wants the grip broken. It meets behind the bar, under the sawed-off.
*Colours: the shutters.*

**The Leaven-Rot sect — Port-au-Levain**
Its heretics are the Spore-Caller, the Groat-Master, the Cellar-Ghoul and the Arch-Fermenter. It wants the Abbot of the Crust.
*Colours: the spore.*

**The pack — BlackPine**
It wants the Chaplain. It answers to the Blood-Moon Howl.
*Colours: the moon.*

To the Company, miasma. To the town, mere-rot.

## Laws of the World

1. Every town has a clock that brings the dead and takes the guilty.
2. The three towns mirror one another, crime for crime and ladder for ladder. Nobody can say why.
3. A rumour that goes unpaid for a day becomes an indictment.
4. Rumour crosses water. Whether it walks, swims or is carried, no one has seen.
5. Wolves flee light.
6. Nobody kills for nothing.
7. The thing on your rack, your plate or your card at dawn is who you are.
8. The law is thin, so the Company is the law.
9. One loaf for each mourner and three for the dead, and every crumb sealed in wax.
10. Debt walks to the mill.
11. Whoever remains when the last lantern goes out inherits.
