 World Codex of Wyrmreach  
Cosmic Origin  
The cosmos began as the First Light, a singular radiant being that shattered into two primal forces: the Sun’s Roaring Anger and the Moon’s Silent Tear. From these celestial events emerged dragons, born of the Sun’s fury, and elves, born of the Moon’s sorrow. Bound by the Threefold Bond—Symbiotic Royal Bond, Ancestral Hybridization, and Twin-Soul Incarnation—dragons and elves share intertwined destinies, resonant magic, and shared origins.  
Central Plot  
Shallock Izladar, a Celestial Elf scholar, ascended into an astral entity after delving into forbidden cosmic texts. His transformation threatened reality, prompting the Luminary Priory and the six elven tribes to imprison him in a realm outside existence, sealed by memory itself. The Remnants of Izladar now seek to erase all memory of him, believing his resurrection will elevate reality. As memories fade, the world decays—forests forget how to grow, stars lose constellations, oceans forget their ecosystems, and dragons and elves weaken. Dragons awaken from dormancy, their elven twins feeling the call, and all paths lead to #Wrymreach, the birthplace of their kind.  
Elven Races  
Wood Elves — Cedarheart Enclave  
Wood Elves live in harmony with nature, skilled in archery and survival. They worship forest spirits and dwell in ancient treehouses. Their olive-toned skin, green or brown hair, and amber or green eyes reflect their woodland origins. Their twin dragon is Sylvan.  
Sky Elves — Galewing Assembly  
Sky Elves are nomadic explorers of the skies, skilled in aerial navigation, music, and astronomy. They venerate sky gods and live on floating platforms and mountaintops. Pale skin, silver or white hair, and blue or gray eyes define them. Their twin dragon is Aetherius.  
Earth Elves — Mosswhisper Tribe  
Earth Elves are craftsmen, miners, and farmers with deep ties to the land. They honor earth deities and ancestral spirits, living in underground cities adorned with gemstones. Their earth-toned skin and soil-colored hair reflect their connection to the land. Their twin dragon is Gaia.  
Water Elves — Tidecaller Circle  
Water Elves are sailors, healers, and navigators of the sea. They worship water spirits and live in coastal or underwater settlements. Their aqua-toned skin, blue or silver hair, and ocean-colored eyes mirror the tides. Their twin dragon is Aquatica.  
Dark Elves — Duskbane Syndicate  
Dark Elves are shadow mages, spies, and assassins dwelling in subterranean labyrinths. They honor night deities and possess obsidian skin, silver or black hair, and violet or crimson eyes. Their twin dragon is Umbra.  
Celestial Elves — Luminary Priory  
Celestial Elves are spiritual beings devoted to enlightenment and cosmic harmony. They meditate upon celestial forces and live in floating crystalline forests. Their luminous skin, silver or gold hair, and starlit eyes reflect their celestial nature. Their twin dragon is Astraia.  
Draconian Races  
Sylvan — Dragon of the Wood Elves  
Sylvan commands nature, shaping plants, healing forests, and communicating with woodland life.  
Aetherius — Dragon of the Sky Elves  
Aetherius wields air magic, shaping winds, calming storms, and sharing telepathic knowledge.  
Gaia — Dragon of the Earth Elves  
Gaia commands geomancy, shaping stone, resonating with crystals, and recalling fossil memories.  
Aquatica — Dragon of the Water Elves  
Aquatica controls water, shaping tides, purifying seas, and communicating with aquatic life.  
Umbra — Dragon of the Dark Elves  
Umbra manipulates shadows, creating illusions, concealing itself, and hunting in darkness.  
Astraia — Dragon of the Celestial Elves  
Astraia channels astral energy, creating shields, projecting celestial beams, and glimpsing cosmic futures.  
Geography  
#Wrymreach  
A cosmic wound where the Sun’s anger and Moon’s tear collided. Reality thins, time folds, and memory bleeds. Wyrm bones hum with ancient resonance. It is the birthplace of dragons and elves.  
The Six Realms  
West: Cedarheart Forest North: Galewing Peaks South: Mosswhisper Depths East: Tidecaller Shores Below: Duskbane Underworld Above: High Realm  
The Astral Expanse  
A realm outside existence, the prison of Shallock Izladar.

Magic System  
Memory-based reality shapes existence; when memory fades, reality decays. Twin-soul magic amplifies spells, senses, and dreams between elves and dragons. Celestial magic draws from Sun and Moon, while elemental magic reflects each tribe’s dragon.  
Factions and Orders
