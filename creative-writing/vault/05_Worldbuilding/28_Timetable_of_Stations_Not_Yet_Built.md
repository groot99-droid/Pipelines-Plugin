# Timetable of Stations Not Yet Built

**THORNE & EXPANSE LINE**
**HOLLOWMERE SPUR**
**Cornelius Thorne, Prop.**

*[A ruled line through the name, in ink, in a second hand. Beside it:]* Prop. ______

HOLLOWMERE — EST. BY THE RAILROAD EXPANSE

---

**TIMETABLE FOR 1894**

In force from Monday, 1 January 1894, until further notice.
Printed at the Eastern Office, November 1893.

No Sunday service.

Connections back east not guaranteed during the present financial disturbance.

*[in a second hand]*

> *Line closed from the 24th. Wire.*

---

## I. STATIONS IN SERVICE

| Station | Down | Up | Remarks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thorne Junction | Day | Dusk | Main line. Connections back east. |
| Widow's Cut | Day | Dusk | Water only. |
| Gallows Wood Siding | — | — | Stops on signal only. |
| HollowMere | Day | Dusk | End of line.† |

† Until the opening of the Extension.

The Daily Train arrives at the start of each Day and departs at Dusk.

> *Thorne Junction. Counted out here.*

> *HollowMere, Up. It does not wait.*

---

## II. EXTENSION OF THE LINE

**UNDER SURVEY. TIMES TO BE ANNOUNCED.**

| Station | Down | Up | Remarks |
|---|---|---|---|
| South-East Halt, for the marsh light | T.B.A. | T.B.A. | Low water only.\* |
| Tin Gallery, the Cave Gate | T.B.A. | T.B.A. | Lamp at the Gate only.§ |
| The Fork | T.B.A. | T.B.A. | Change here. Two roads.‖ |
| Harrow, for timber | — | — | Under survey; may not be built.¶ |
|  | — | — | Stops on signal only.‡ |

\* Passengers will consult the tide. The Company does not keep it.
§ The Company does not say why.
‖ A passenger at the Fork travels one road. The ticket reads both. The Company does not say which.
¶ Owner of record: Harrow.
‡ Name to be announced.

> *South-East Halt. Light lit at Dusk. Seen from the step.*

> *The Gate. Two out of the cave this month.*
> *Drag marks.*
> *Black on them. The Undertaker would not write it as blood.*
> *Ruled the cause through.*

> *The Fork. Either road is in force. Neither is guaranteed.*

*[Against Harrow, the second hand writes nothing.]*

> *The last row.*
> *One fare, January, one-way. Booked. Not punched.*
> *He sat the bench an hour before the Rush. In case it came early.*
> *Held for the passenger.*

*[Pasted against the Harrow row, a little askew, typed:]*

1952. GROUNDS 99. PAYROLL TRAIN, FRIDAYS. PRODUCTION IS OBLIGATION.

*[Beneath it, the same typewriter:]*

SITE ███, N. DAK. SEALED CAR ONLY. NO RETURN WORKING.

*[Pasted half over the first slip, so that its type shows at the edge: a slip of some other stuff, not paper, faded, one corner gone:]*

2626. The same place. No train. The grade holds as far as where the trestle was. Numbers where names were.

---

## III. RULES OF CARRIAGE, 1894

Passengers, consignees and servants of the Company will observe the following.

1. The Daily Train arrives at the start of each Day and departs at Dusk. It does not wait.
2. Freight is carried at the consignee's risk. The Company counts the crate, not the contents.
3. As 1893.
4. Coffins ride in Car 3, the back car, and are not opened in transit, by any person, for any reason.
5. As 1893.
6. Persons of rank and their escorts travel in the private car and are not entered on the passenger count.
7. Mail sealed in black wax is the property of the addressee and of the Company. The Conductor does not read it.
8. As 1893.
9. A passenger booked in is a passenger counted out.
10. As 1893.
11. As 1893.
12. Should the Conductor of record be unable to sign, the manifest is signed by whoever remains.

> *6. Dogs: counted.*

> *7. The wax still comes.*

*[Against Rule 12, the second hand underlines "whoever remains" twice and writes nothing.]*

**For the Extension:**

13. South-East Halt is served at low water only. The Company does not keep the tide.
14. One lamp is shown at the Gate, and none between the Tin Gallery and the Gate. The Company is not answerable for what is lost on that section, in the cars or out of them.
15. At the Fork a passenger travels one road. Tickets are not exchanged.
16. Fares are not sold to stations under survey. A passenger for such a station travels as freight, at the consignee's risk.

> *16. Then who signs for them.*

By order,
The Thorne & Expanse Line

> *Conductor of record:*

‡ Name to be announced.
