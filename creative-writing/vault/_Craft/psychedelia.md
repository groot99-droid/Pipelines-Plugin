# Psychedelia

## What the register means here

Perception dissolves and reconstitutes. **The self is the variable; the world is what the self is made of.** The register's state column is `Self`:

`intact < permeable < fractured < dissolved < reconstituted`

It is not monotone (the self comes back), it must reach `fractured`, and after the fracture the way out must be **a surrender or a choice, not a fix**. That last rule is the vault's own, from two works: "Through deliberate effort, the mind redirected itself." (Trip Tracker, Phase V) and "Choosing to be you" ([[07_Poems_and_Prose/01_Trips_Are_Like_Exes|Trips Are Like Exes]]).

## What the vault does

**It has one template and has not used it.** [[09_Dream_Journal/02_Trip_Tracker_-_July_Fourteenth_2023|Trip Tracker]] is the only piece that records the experience, and it says on its face that it is meant for reuse: "This Vision can be used as: A mythic origin for a character's awakening. A symbolic map for journeys into the subconscious. A template for visionary experiences in narrative worlds. A psychological trial or rite of passage." Its seven phases (Awakening, Expansion, Fracturing, Descent, Reorientation, Encounter, Dissolution of Memory) are the vault's only structural model of a psychedelic arc. The Creative Codex lists "Ritual Mechanics" as a frontier and asks "How do repetition, initiation, and boundary-making generate transformation?", and the annotation of that essay calls the Tracker "raw case-study material". Nothing else uses it: the only files that refer to it are the vault index, the folder index, its own annotation, and the annotation of the Codex essay. No story in the vault runs on its phases.

**It rarely says the word.** "Psychedelic" occurs once in the whole vault, in the Tracker's frontmatter (`genre: psychedelic-record`). The experience is otherwise written as "the Vision", "ego dissolution" ("Your ego dissolved, your inner self bare", Trips Are Like Exes), and as perception itself. The Tracker is explicit about what it is not: "Rather than focusing on substances or their mechanics" and "without glorifying or encouraging the circumstances that produced it". That restraint is the register's ethical rule (below).

**Perception is the landscape.** The works adjacent to the Tracker do not narrate a trip; they build a world out of a sense. [[03_Stories/03_The_Yellow_Drop_and_The_Plain_Before_Sound|The Yellow Drop]] builds a world out of sound ("This was not light painting a scene. This was sound building one."). [[03_Stories/01_A_Journey_Into_the_Soul|A Journey Into the Soul]] makes an organ a planet ("What if the human eye wasn't an organ, but a planet?"). [[03_Stories/02_A_Journey_Into_the_Heartstream_-_The_Rite_of_the_Blue-Born|The Rite of the Blue-Born]] is a rite of passage through the body that ends in separation: "No one stays together. Everyone is carried away." And the Tracker's own imagery is the sense organ made cosmic: "an immense eye with the sun as its pupil".

**Recognition is the ending.** Journey into the Soul: "The journey ends not with revelation, but with recognition". Heartstream's rite is called "The Recognition". The Dream Journal: "A dream. A vow. A recognition." Its annotation files the Journey's ending as `tidy-epiphany-ending`, in contrast with the horror endings ([[_Annotations/03_Stories/01_A_Journey_Into_the_Soul]]). In this register that is a native ending, not a defect.

## What the vault lacks

- **A comparable description.** The Tracker's annotation names `seven-phase-structuring`, `mythic-third-person-distancing` and `symbolic-glossary`. It describes the *list* of phases. Nothing in the vault describes what the phases *do to each other*.
- **A tag that fits.** The Tracker's whole file is one chunk, tagged `plot=exposition, codex-worldbuilding-aside`. The chunk tagger asked for `archetypal-guide-encounter` and `altered-perception` for it, and for `sound-as-substance` on The Yellow Drop, and was refused each time because the vocabulary is closed. Those suggestions are adopted in this batch.
- **A causal reading.** A phase list is a taxonomy. The ledger below shows the Tracker has an actual chain under the taxonomy, and that the chain has the same shape as a well-made story.

The chain alternates cleanly (T, T, B, T, T, B), and it has the two things the register asks for. **The valence flip** is row 4, a but that reaches back past the row before it: the barriers that dissolved as connection in row 2 are the same barriers that now mean exposure. The mechanism did not change; its meaning did. And **the turn** is row 5: the escalation is not defeated by a fix but by a deliberate yielding. Row 7 is a but too: the return takes back nearly everything the encounter gave.

#### Causal ledger — Trips Are Like Exes

The same chain, compressed into a prose poem, in a different mode. Note the ending: the reconstitution here is complete. That is the difference between the two vault examples, and it is why the register admits both `return-with-residue` and `self-reclaimed`.

Mode: confessional-poetry
Register: psychedelic
Expect: pass

| # | Link | Because | Beat | Response | Self | Changes |
|---|------|---------|------|----------|------|---------|
| 1 | open | - | Trips are like exes: we blame the trip when it goes wrong, love the memories, would not go back, and run and hide from the truth like Jekyll and Hyde. | denies | intact | We keep taking another trip, calling it finding ourselves while looking for others. |
| 2 | T | 1 "another trip" | We take another trip, another chance, and every trip has a story with two sides. | chooses | permeable | Every story has two sides, and we go anyway. |
| 3 | T | 2 "two sides" | After the trip you were broken and lost. | none | fractured | You are broken and lost, with your ego coming apart. |
| 4 | B | 3 "broken and lost" | Your ego dissolved, your inner self bare. The moment you thought would bring ultimate peace was the moment you lost what you loved. | none | dissolved | Peace turned out to be the loss of what you loved. |
| 5 | T | 4 "the loss of what you loved" | Freed from your ego, you yearn for your life; for a moment your mind is forced outside itself. | none | dissolved | Free of the ego, you want your life back. |
| 6 | T | 5 "want your life back" | Your self collects back to you like a pool of identity in the shards of the after-party. Cleaning up is you coming back, "Choosing to be you". | chooses | reconstituted | You choose to be you, and every sliver comes back. |

Ending: self-reclaimed

Row 4 is the same valence flip as the Tracker's: peace and loss are the same event. Row 6 is the same turn: a choice, not a fix. The vault's two psychedelic pieces differ only in what the return costs, and the register is built so a writer must decide.

## Guidance for writing in this register

- **Write the experience's structure and symbols. Never substances, doses, sourcing or how-to.** This is the vault's own precedent, from the Tracker, and it is a restraint on the piece, not on the theme. The drafter is told this and the self-revision stage checks for it.
- **The phases are causal.** Intensification (T), a valence flip in which what opened you now exposes you (B), a surrender that answers it (T), a return that loses most of what was found (B).
- **Perception is the landscape.** Let a sense organ be the setting: sound builds the world, the eye is a planet.
- **End on residue, recognition, or a chosen reassembly.** Not on an explanation of what it meant. The horror mode forbids `self-reclaimed`; combine this register with it and the mode wins.

## The voice these pieces share, and an open question

The register-bearing pieces share a sentence-level device that appears across at least five of them: **the negation ladder**, a run of "not" lines that ends on the true thing. "Not dangerous. / Not dramatic. / Just wrong." (Missing Campsites); "Not an earthquake. / Not a storm. / Not an enemy." then a squirrel (People of the Bark); "Not a dramatic revelation. / Not a career decision. / Not a motivational speech." then "A dream. A vow. A recognition." (Dream Journal); and the two-line form: "This is not metaphor. / This is fieldwork." (A Journey Into the Soul), "This was not light painting a scene. / This was sound building one." (The Yellow Drop). Several of them also share a clinical or field-manual distance ("The Vision occurred"; "This is not metaphor. / This is fieldwork."), present tense (Missing Campsites, People of the Bark, The Yellow Drop, A Journey Into the Soul), capitalised names for phases (the Tracker), and an ending on recognition or an echo.

Four of those five are marked `unclassified` (Dream Journal, People of the Bark, A Journey Into the Soul, The Yellow Drop; the fifth, Missing Campsites, is horror-prose), so for most of them the pipeline still has no voice to give. **That is an author decision, not a fix**: whether to promote this cluster to a fifth mode (working name `visionary-record`), leave it unclassified and ask per piece as [[CLAUDE.md]] says, or fold its structure into the registers only, as done here. Nothing in this batch adds a mode.
