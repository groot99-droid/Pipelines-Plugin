# Trips Are Like Exes

**Type:** Prose poem  
**Source:** `Master_Volume_1`, lines 2858–2863  
**Text:** complete and verbatim — nothing cut, condensed, or rewritten.  
**Note:** Untitled in source; title taken from the opening line.  

---
Trips are like exes— We’d like to think it’s all their fault when it goes wrong, Love the memories, but wouldn’t ever go back. Yet we take another trip, another chance, Finding ourselves while we are out looking for others. Turns out maybe every trip has a story, And every story has two sides.  
Like Jekyll and Hyde, we run and we hide from the truth. Life’s ugly, love’s got a metallic aftertaste, But in the end, we’d drink poison to feel more alive. We flirt with death at every chance for celebration— Birth, betrothal, or betrayal, we always celebrate. That’s what life’s about: To plan the party, crash it, and clean up after it. Because for fuck's sake, we’ve only got today.  
But the most beautiful part Is finding yourself after a trip. You were broken and lost, Your ego dissolved, your inner self bare. The moment that you thought would bring the ultimate peace Was actually just the moment you lost what you loved.  
When you are freed from your ego, you yearn for your life. Not that you are in any peril, But for a moment, your mind is forced outside itself. Then comes that moment when your self collects back to you, Like a pool of identity in the shards of the after-party. That cleaning up is you coming back, Choosing to be you, And watching as every sliver that is you comes back.

“In the end we’d love to think it’s all beautiful or ugly but instead the ugly is fucking beauty and every beauties got an ugly side” PART 1
