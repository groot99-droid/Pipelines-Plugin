# Darkness

**Type:** Poem  
**Source:** `Master_Volume_1`, lines 5779–5808  
**Text:** complete and verbatim — nothing cut, condensed, or rewritten.  

---
Darkness

To answer shadow with shadow  
is to feed the very night you wish to end.  
Strike for strike, wound for wound—  
the cycle closes in,  
a serpent devouring its own tail.

Darkness does not starve  
when given more of itself.  
It swells,  
it thickens,  
it learns your name.

Only those who have walked its corridors  
and felt its cold breath on their skin  
can choose to turn,  
to lift a lantern instead of a blade.

Light is not the absence of shadow—  
it is the defiance of it.  
It is the hand that refuses to strike,  
the voice that refuses to echo hate.

And when light is offered  
where darkness once reigned,  
the night does not vanish—  
but it begins to retreat,  
step by reluctant step,  
until the horizon remembers the sun.
