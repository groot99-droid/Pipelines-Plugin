# Taking All From the Rich

**Type:** Poem  
**Source:** `Master_Volume_2`, lines 83–91  
**Text:** complete and verbatim — nothing cut, condensed, or rewritten.  
**Note:** Untitled in source; title taken from the first line.  

---
Taking all from the rich, to give to the poor  
Is the same as taking from the poor, to give the rich more.  
The world doesn’t need another Robin Hood,  
Just someone who’s willing to do something good.

For though King John wore the villain's crown,  
Two wrongs can’t make a right; they just drag us down.  
In the end, it’s a dirge we all must face,  
A cycle of heartache in an endless race.
