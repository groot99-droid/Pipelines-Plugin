# Hopscotch

*chalked under the lantern on the square*

One for the bell, and the bell brings the town.
Alfie counted the gallows stairs: thirteen up, and none come down.
Alfie climbed in a crate on the morning train.
The crate went east. It never wrote.

  *Ten chalk squares and ten of us.*
  *Hop the stone and don't look down.*

Two for the pump, two hands on the iron.
Nell pumped first and Nell drank first.
Fever by Friday. Ribbons, blue.
Nell rode the back car east.

  *Ten chalk squares and ten of us.*
  *Hop the stone and don't look down.*

Three for the lantern we chalk it under.
It keeps the wolves off. It keeps us in.
Sid chased his shadow to where the light stops.
Where does the light stop?
Sid knew. His cap's on the fence.

  *Ten chalk squares and ten of us.*
  *Hop the stone and don't look down.*

Four for the Lantern, sign and all.
The barman carried it into the mere.
Flo swept the floor for a penny a night
and finished the cups when Mrs Grieves wasn't looking.
Something in a cup was not sugar.
Flo's broom is still by the door.

  *Ten chalk squares and ten of us.*
  *Hop the stone and don't look down.*

Five for the oven, up before light,
ten plates on the wake table, laid out right.
Peg stole the heel off every loaf.
One for each mourner and three for the dead:
three on Peg's plate,
and nobody took the heel.

  *Ten chalk squares and ten of us.*
  *Hop the stone and don't look down.*

Six for the manor, where the clock stopped
at eleven forty-seven and stayed.
Ted wound his gran's clock every night
and told the time by the train.
Rent comes due when the sun's straight up.
Ted went to the mill to pay it with his hands.
He's at the loft window. He doesn't wave.

  *Ten chalk squares and ten of us.*
  *Hop the stone and don't look down.*

Seven for the well, and the ladder down.
Winnie dropped pebbles and counted the splash.
Winnie threw her stone and the stone went in.
Winnie went down the ladder to fetch it.
We counted for her.
We are counting still.

  *Ten chalk squares and ten of us.*
  *Hop the stone and don't look down.*

Eight for the chapel and the Company bell.
Lil sang loudest, always a beat ahead.
Lil went up to the rope to ring it in.
The hymn came out one voice short.
There's a glove in the rope.
Nobody says whose.

  *Ten chalk squares and ten of us.*
  *Hop the stone and don't look down.*

Nine for the pines, and the pines aren't empty.
May was scared of the dark,
so May walked toward every light.
There was a light in the fen. May walked.
Who's holding the lantern out there in the fen?

  *Ten chalk squares and ten of us.*
  *Hop the stone and don't look down.*

Who chalks it fresh when we're all in bed?

But the tenth square isn't chalk.

Ten for the stone with tomorrow's name.
I hopped every one of them. That's the rule:
you never land where the stone is lying.
Alfie. Nell. Sid. Flo. Peg.
Ted. Winnie. Lil. May.
I hid. I watched. I remember.
I can read. I read it.
I won't read it out.

  *Ten chalk squares and one of me.*
  *Throw your stone. It's your turn now.*

Nobody's out in HollowMere.
Everybody's next.
