Personal letters — direct, unsent or confessional correspondence.

- [[08_Letters/01_Dear_Katie|Dear Katie]] — an amends letter reckoning with a past relationship's harm and the author's own role in it → annotation: [[_Annotations/08_Letters/01_Dear_Katie|notes]]
- [[08_Letters/02_Five_Letters_at_the_Fork|Five Letters at the Fork]] — five unsent letters, one for each townsfolk trade on the night it reaches its evolution: Tamsin Crumb to the dead Aldous Vane, the Postmaster to his sister who cannot read, the Undertaker to Father Bray, the Bartender to the drowned barman (written twice, one for the stove), and the Company Clerk to Ivy Cotter the night after the Baron falls, with the writ in his drawer → annotation: [[_Annotations/08_Letters/02_Five_Letters_at_the_Fork|notes]]
- [[08_Letters/03_Censored|Censored]] — three letters out of Grounds 99 under Standing Order 14, in their file copies with the struck matter still legible: Foreman Hask to his daughter, Ada Kerrow at the Forge to the brother who named her, and the Collier at the kilns to the mother of Tony, whose name is on the board and whose lamp still burns, a letter held and never sent → annotation: [[_Annotations/08_Letters/03_Censored|notes]]
- [[08_Letters/04_Black_Wax|Black Wax]] — five pigeon letters of the Black-Crust Contract: in 1788 a Gallery house buys a death from slot forty, and an old Apprenti accepts; a Plongeur, Bastien Leclou, writes to his sister and is found at his station with his hands in the water; in 1793 his sister, now the Committee's Secretary, sends the same bird to the same slot against the house of Maillard, and the slot-holder's last letter, never sent, says he has chosen twice → annotation: [[_Annotations/08_Letters/04_Black_Wax|notes]]
- [[08_Letters/05_One_Shot|One Shot]] — three letters, 4–6 December 1893: the Ranger gives her one white-threaded arrest bolt to the stood-down Watchman and writes to the Magistrate of the Territory why; the Watchman writes to the dead Hattie, in six numbered steps, how before dawn he spent the bolt on a wolf that had Agnes by the arm at the pump ("One shot, and the whole square went quiet"); the Gamekeeper hangs the wolf from the gallows beam, where the crows take its eyes by noon, and writes to the dead Aldous Vane his standing order for his own one shot → annotation: [[_Annotations/08_Letters/05_One_Shot|notes]]
