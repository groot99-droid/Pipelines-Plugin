# Shoot for the Moon

**Type:** Aphorisms and reflections  
**Source:** `Master_Volume_2`, lines 116–147  
**Text:** complete and verbatim — nothing cut, condensed, or rewritten.  
**Note:** Untitled in source; title taken from the central image.

Isn’t it funny? Just How mothers seem to have the most beautiful sayings for the dullest questions? Maybe it’s cause they have all the answers, or eyes in the back of your head? Well I’ll tell you this much, for more moments than we can recall they watched us grow observing our reactions to life just to formulate the perfect solution for the obstacles they saw in their future. A good father can make a man but an attentive mother no matter the flaws can make the most beautiful people.

My beloved mother with all her perfections and flaws once told me more times than I can count; that the stars are yours for the taking just shoot for the moon. A cheap twist on an old and popular quote if you ask most, but if you ask me there is no deeper pool of wisdom that could produce a saying so impactful. I speculated in my earlier understandings that if I shot for the moon i would inevitably miss the mark and fly endlessly into the void. However I don’t believe it could be proposed as an act of chance or a streak of luck; that when i finally did launch myself into the void aiming so far from the moon that i had no directory in sight. I realized in the depths of uncertainty opportunity shines brightest. So maybe instead of waiting to see the light of a dawn that is not promised reminisce among the stars. 

For so long i feared just how deep the undertow of change would suck me down into the abyss of darkness that we so believe is waiting for us. Yet here we are afraid to try and relocate ourselves to a more suitable environment while the one we are in promises much less than which we so quickly regard.

Anytime you allow yourself to build up a negative how daunting falling into 
but i never took into account that all I accomplishing in my fight against vulnerability was chasing dark side of the moon

Often times we would rather live a life mediocrity fearing that we will land to far outside our definition of success  
To realize we are already slipping through the cracks in the foundation of existence

Our soul is a flower that modern systems seek to extinguish  
For if a field of wild flowers stands before the teeth of the tiller of revolution, inevitably they are crushed

Sacrifice the beauty of a thousand small groups for one homogenous group that represents their history  
Silence the voices around the campfire and pour in the advertising of new infomercials

Society isn’t broken it just has the wrong goal in mind, we have to become apart of the change we seek  
Counting on your neighbors to pull their weight before you is pending your success on a chance that is never promised

Waiting for others to change is the system that has failed the world we are in now  
We hold our neighbors accountable but not our own household, we chastise instead of reaching out

Yet we wonder why we are sick, we can’t give someone else the cure and not use it yourself  
We spread the wave of negativity every time we push away a positive thought

Seize not just the moment but the narrative in witch your story is told, 
why wait for others to tell the story you already know by heart

Yes the constant tracing of the wound that is self improvement never stops hurting  
But your heart beats a little stronger each time you extend or receive a warm gesture
