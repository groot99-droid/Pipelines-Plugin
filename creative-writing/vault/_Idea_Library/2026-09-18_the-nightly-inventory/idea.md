**Working title:** The Nightly Inventory

**Premise:** A night-shift lighthouse keeper starts a written log of small
wrongnesses around the station — a light that dims a second early, a gull
that won't land, a door found unlatched. Each one is explainable alone,
until the list itself becomes the thing that's wrong. The keeper cannot
stop adding to it even as the log starts to feel like it's counting down
to something, and the piece ends without rescue: either the keeper
becomes the next entry in someone else's inventory, or the log begins
writing entries no one added.

**Candidate themes:** isolation, routine as denial, the log as both
defense and trap, escalation-by-catalogued-incident

**Candidate archetypes:** the watcher/keeper; the counted thing (empty at
first, revealed as antagonist by the end)

**Project tie:** none named — do not infer one.
