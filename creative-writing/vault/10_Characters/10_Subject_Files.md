## SF-1

**SUBJECT ████ — designation THE TIMBER-WOLF**

**Former name:** ██████████████████████

**Billet:** Retained on the bunkhouse rack. Answers roll.

**Trial:** W ("Reversion"). Week 1 from Mon 13 Oct 1952.
- Exposure: weeks 1 to 5. Completed.
- Isolation: weeks 3 to 8. Completed.
- The treeline as enclosure: from week 4. Continuing.
- Withdrawal of speech: week 9. See Observation.
- [line withheld]

**Associates:** Four, by observed rank, as entered by camp staff: Timber-Stalker. Pit-Fiend. Den-Keeper. Alpha-Born. Former names not taken. All four were on the roll at the start of the season. All four are on the roll now.

**Point of contact:** Rev. Elias Moss, chapel hut. Resin blessings at the gate, mornings; see attached.
[Nothing is attached.]
Point of contact: not at work, Tue 4 Nov.
Collar: not recovered. Observed in use, at the gate, from Mon 10 Nov.

**Housing:** The hollow pine, Blackpine Woods, below the felling slope, on the wolf loop. Secondary: the drowned adit, west of the ford, through to the Deep Level. As assigned.

**Condition at last observation:**
- Jaw: lengthened. Pushed forward of the face by the width of two fingers. Will not close square.
- Teeth: crowded to the front. Four lost. The gaps have not closed.
- Hair: coarse, grey-black. Down the spine, the backs of the hands, the tops of the feet.
- Nails: thickened, split, black at the root.
- Gait: on four at night from week 8. Stands for roll. Did not stand on Mon 15 Dec.
- Weight: gaining. Ration: refused. Source of intake: not established.

**Property envelope:**
1. Spectacles, steel frame, one lens cracked across.
2. Pencil, a stub, chewed at the end.
3. Seed catalogue, nursery stock, spring 19██. Apple varieties ticked in pencil: eleven. Two ticked twice.

**Subject statement (verbatim):** "Leave the window."
Requested in writing four times. Four requests on file. Window: not applicable after week 4.

**Observation:** At roll the subject named every tree he could see, aloud: the species, then the height in feet, then how old he thought it was. He had the species right. The orderly checked. By week 8 he said the names through a jaw that no longer met, and they came out wet and short. Week 8: orderly ████ bitten, left forearm, to the bone, when he reached in with the tin plate. Orderly transferred. Week 9: *Speech: withdrawn.* Week 10: the subject took the spectacles out of the envelope when it was left on the bench, and held them up to where his face had been.

RESULT: anomalous. CAUSE: unknown.

## SF-2

**SUBJECT ████ — designation THE CRAWLER MOTHER**

**Former name:** Mrs ████████

**Billet:** Struck. Slot ██ reissued.

**Trial:** D ("Immersion / breath-hold").

| Series | Interval | Recoveries | Non-recoveries |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ██ | 14 | 0 |
| 2 | ██ | 31 | 2 |
| 3 | ██ | 40 | 9 |
| 4 | ██ | 1 | — |

Interval figures withheld. Series 4: subject only.

**Associates:** Subjects of Trial D not surfacing for roll. Nine, then eleven. The camp calls them "the Tunnel Crawlers". The file has no other word for them.

**Housing:** The flooded stope, the Deep Level. Two plank walkways. The sump. As assigned.
Lamp in refuge: maintain.

**Condition at last observation:**
- Height: up 5 in. since intake. The length is in the spine and the forearms.
- Fingers: lengthened past the old knuckle line. Nails soft, peeling.
- Skin: water-pale. The blue-white of a hand left in a basin overnight. Wrinkled to the shoulder, and it does not smooth.
- Hair: gone, in handfuls, by Series 3.
- Ribs: every one showing. The chest deep and barrel-wide over them.
- Eyes: filmed grey. Open under water.
- Respiration: one breath per ██ minutes, at rest.
- Mouth: lips gone bloodless. Speaks in a whisper with no breath behind it.

**Property envelope:**
1. Hair ribbon, blue, frayed at one end.
2. Thimble, brass, dented on the crown.
3. Photograph, one. Face redacted per standing order. On the reverse, in pencil: a date, and a height in inches.

**Subject statement (verbatim):** "Has a letter come."
Entered weekly, Mon 6 Oct to Mon 15 Dec: eleven entries. Answered each week: *No correspondence.*

**Observation:** Under the walkways she had the others hold hands, so they could count together without speaking. The squeeze went down the line and back. When a hand went slack she held it to the end of the count anyway, then let it go into the sump. Staff began calling her Mother. Designation amended to reflect usage. In Series 4 the thimble was found on the walkway, worn on the longest finger, which it no longer fitted past the first joint.

RESULT: anomalous. CAUSE: unknown.

## SF-3

**SUBJECT ████ — designation THE SLEEPLESS**

**Former name:** ████

**Billet:** Struck from the bunkhouse rack, Mon 6 Oct. Slot ██ reissued.

**Trial:** S ("Wakefulness schedule").

| Day | Status | Lung-Rot | Tonic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | awake | slight | issued |
| 14 | awake | moderate | issued |
| 30 | awake | moderate | withheld per schedule |
| 47 | awake | marked | withheld per schedule |
| 62 | awake | marked | withheld per schedule |
| Total | not entered | | |

**Housing:** The Bunkhouse root cellar, under the bunk room. The hatch is at the end bunk. As assigned.

**Condition at last observation:**
- Lids: thinned to paper and drawn back to the brow. Do not close. Have not closed since day 30.
- Eyes: dry. Red to the rim. Cracked at the corners, and they weep and crust and weep.
- Weight: 96 lb. At intake: 171 lb.
- Face: skin tight over the cheekbones. The teeth show at rest.
- Hands: tremor, constant. Nails bitten to the quick, then past it.
- Cough: on the hour, every hour, through the boards.
- Speech: clear. Always clear.

**Property envelope:**
1. Wristwatch, stopped at ten past four.
2. Prayer card, edges gone soft from handling.
3. Envelope, sealed, addressed in the subject's own hand: *To be read when I sleep.* Unopened.

**Subject statement (verbatim):** "Count me."

**Observation:** At the Midnight Roll Call the whistle goes, and the men have fifteen seconds to stand by their bunks. Under the boards, the subject answers to his old number, which is another man's now. Then, under his breath, he gives the roll for the men sleeping above him, 0 to 9, and one number more. Men in the end bunks have asked to be moved. Request: noted. Through the gap in the hatch the subject can be seen looking up. He does not blink, because he cannot.
Orderly's entry: *Subject is not on the roll. Subject does not accept this.*

RESULT: anomalous. CAUSE: unknown.

**Housing:** The mess hall, after roll call. As assigned.

Since then, when one of the twelve coughs, the other eleven cough. When one stumbled on the mess-hall step, eleven went down on the same knee.
After roll call the mess hall is cleared of staff. The humming continues without the whistle.
