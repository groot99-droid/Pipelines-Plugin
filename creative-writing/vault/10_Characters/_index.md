Character profiles — a barbarian raider archetype, plus four wandering mystic-philosopher archetypes each modeled on a real-world thinker.

- [[10_Characters/01_Graknox_the_Ironbound_Raider|Graknox the Ironbound Raider]] — a stoic northern raider-warrior built from clan sayings and story hooks → annotation: [[_Annotations/10_Characters/01_Graknox_the_Ironbound_Raider|notes]]
- [[10_Characters/02_The_Bottonian_Philosopher|The Bottonian Philosopher]] — a de Botton-styled wandering sage of emotional fragility and everyday melancholy → annotation: [[_Annotations/10_Characters/02_The_Bottonian_Philosopher|notes]]
- [[10_Characters/03_The_Kafkan_Seer|The Kafkan Seer]] — a Kafka-styled seer of paradox, cages, and the frozen sea within → annotation: [[_Annotations/10_Characters/03_The_Kafkan_Seer|notes]]
- [[10_Characters/04_The_Darwishan_Exile|The Darwishan Exile]] — a Darwish-styled poet-exile of homeland, memory, and incurable hope → annotation: [[_Annotations/10_Characters/04_The_Darwishan_Exile|notes]]
- [[10_Characters/05_The_Rossettian_Oracle|The Rossettian Oracle]] — a Rossetti-styled oracle binding love, beauty, and mortality → annotation: [[_Annotations/10_Characters/05_The_Rossettian_Oracle|notes]]
- [[10_Characters/06_The_Confession_Book_of_St_Brannock_s|The Confession Book of St Brannock's]] — the new Vicar's confession book, 17 October to 6 December 1893: one numbered page for every soul who came to him, in a fixed form of look, name as given, carries, wants, confessed, where they stand and seal, holding all twenty role cards as people of the town, a margin hand nobody owns, a leaf cut away, his own page, and a blank page 21 → annotation: [[_Annotations/10_Characters/06_The_Confession_Book_of_St_Brannock_s|notes]]
- [[10_Characters/07_Four_Notices|Four Notices]] — the Constable's four wanted notices of December 1893 (the Zealot, the Activist, the Bandit and the Hitman), each a Company front that names the deed and leaves the name blank, and each turned overnight so that the square reads a back in another hand: the candle-keeper, a railyard sleeper, Tamsin Crumb, and the one who burned the black-wax letter → annotation: [[_Annotations/10_Characters/07_Four_Notices|notes]]
- [[10_Characters/08_Persons_of_Note|Persons of Note]] — the Postmaster's who-to-ask guide for persons arriving by the Daily Train, handed across the sorting table on 11 December 1893: the landmark figures, those who keep terms, the law, the house and the wood and those who come by train, each with where to find them, what they ask, what they give, what not to say and what post they get, under the Clerk's pencilled approvals, and ending on the persons not entered → annotation: [[_Annotations/10_Characters/08_Persons_of_Note|notes]]
- [[10_Characters/09_The_Tally_Rack|The Tally Rack]] — the yard's Tally Rack roll and grain-density register for the Dawn Tribunal of Saturday 20 December 1952: ten billets weighed for the camp's ten offices and trades, their brands, debts, sins and forks, and a rack that comes out 3 lb 6 oz heavy because an oak billet stands in a pine slot, with "A.K." under the brand of slot 8 → annotation: [[_Annotations/10_Characters/09_The_Tally_Rack|notes]]
- [[10_Characters/10_Subject_Files|Subject Files]] — Anomaly Unknown's subject files for Grounds 99, compiled 20 December 1952 and stamped RETAIN UNTIL 2626: the four bosses (the Timber-Wolf, the Crawler Mother, the Sleepless, the Choir) each with a trial, a housing and a condition at last observation, an extract of the acquisition procedure that breaks off, Dr Lowell Ambry with nothing found after him, and a blank form → annotation: [[_Annotations/10_Characters/10_Subject_Files|notes]]
- [[10_Characters/11_The_Density_Audit|The Density Audit]] — the Master Proof-Reader's night register of Tuesday 8 September 1789: ten platters weighed for the Hall's ten offices, from Le Levain of the house of Vane to platter 9, Yann Le Goff called Petit-Yann, a dockhand who works the dish pit; the Gallery's bread is declared and never weighed, the rack comes out four onces heavy, and a stolen Counting-House seal is found in the one loaf with no thumb-press in it, the fine unpaid at eleven in the morning → annotation: [[_Annotations/10_Characters/11_The_Density_Audit|notes]]
- [[10_Characters/12_Pledged_and_Unredeemed|Pledged and Unredeemed]] — Harold, the oldest man on the Grant, keeps a pawn counter behind his step and has never sold a pledge; when a Company writ of 15 December forces a forfeit sale for 23 December, he writes up every ticket's owner so the town knows what it buys (Ivy's lantern, the Drifter's tin knife, Horace's study key, the lake man's watch lent to the Vicar, the lost Detective's notebook, Flo's skeleton key, Gert's stolen jet brooch, a sealed alibi paper, Wenna's chalk, the Watchman's irons and empty-looped crossbow, the Gamekeeper's fowling piece with its shot untaken, Moll's second bottle held with no number); the sale goes item by item, the Clerk buys the study key, and at dusk Harold hands Flo's key to a stranger holding her half-ticket, by his own rule → annotation: [[_Annotations/10_Characters/12_Pledged_and_Unredeemed|notes]]
- [[10_Characters/13_The_Two_Roads|The Two Roads]] — the story-mode cast arcs as fifteen cards in three suits read on three nights by three readers who never know of each other: the island's masters read for their apprentices on a salt-cod crate on Saint John's Eve 1789; HollowMere's keepers read by Wenna with the Lantern's house pack on Christmas Eve 1893, the first night with no train; Grounds 99's trusties read for the Head Tallyman from a dead man's fifty-one-card deck on 17 December 1952; each card a face and two roads, given as fortunes and never as fates → annotation: [[_Annotations/10_Characters/13_The_Two_Roads|notes]]
