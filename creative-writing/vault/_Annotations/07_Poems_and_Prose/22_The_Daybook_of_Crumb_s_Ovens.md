Wikilink back to [[07_Poems_and_Prose/22_The_Daybook_of_Crumb_s_Ovens|The Daybook of Crumb's Ovens]].

The confessional mode in prose-poem form, organised by the four back rooms (cold room, mixing room, oven room, proofing room). Each section is headed by its room and by which clock it keeps: the Company's on the wall, the island's in the crock. Recipe lines are written as confession ("one spoon of yesterday… Stir on the island's hour, not the mainland's"), so the instructions carry the grief. The arithmetic of the island's wake custom (one loaf to every mourner and three to each of the dead) against "one sack and the sweepings" is the poem's pressure, and the dead (Dora Finch, Wilf, Percy) are the wave-1 Train manifest's shipment of 21 October seen from the ovens. "I sweep every night. That is my alibi, and it is theirs" settles the cleaned-up-crime-scene trope as complicity without accusation. The Rumour Loaves (unsigned papers that reach her under coins, under the door, once inside a returned glove) make the bakery the town's rumour mill and leave every author unnamed. Read alongside Tamsin's chapters in *Tie by Tie*, it is the founding legend's baker forty years on. No tie is asserted. Plot logic: not applicable, poem form.
