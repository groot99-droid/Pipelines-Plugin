Wikilink back to [[07_Poems_and_Prose/03_Am_I_Me_Enough|Am I Me Enough]].

The poem's structural spine is a repeated triad ("mistook endurance for virtue, / mistook sacrifice for proof, / mistook my own breaking / for a sign") that it uses at least three times to build and then dismantle the same faulty logic — proving worth through suffering — until the speaker can name the pattern outright. The real turn happens at "Every goodbye felt like a verdict... / Was I ever enough for anyone / if I was never enough for myself," which reframes the poem's own title question as the wrong question, a move it repeats near the close with "So was I good enough? / Maybe that was the wrong question." The anaphoric "who no longer begs... who no longer trades... who no longer mistakes" section marks the recovery stage in the same triadic rhythm the poem used for the wound, so healing is stated in the same grammatical shape as the harm, just inverted. It ends on the shortest, plainest line in the piece — "because I was mine" — which lands harder for following forty-plus lines of longer, winding syntax.
