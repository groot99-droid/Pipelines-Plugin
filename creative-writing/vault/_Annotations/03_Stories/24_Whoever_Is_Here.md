Wikilink back to [[03_Stories/24_Whoever_Is_Here|Whoever Is Here]].

The Hitman's vendetta, and the vault's settlement of "the chosen one" (tired), subverted. Its premise is the game's: no one is dealt the Hitman, and a random stream drops the black wax into a passive player's mail. The story's form is a list headed *Reasons*, written in pencil on the back of the last letter anyone sent "you", four winters ago, kept for the name on the outside. The ledger has ten rows, four therefores and five buts, and each reason is a shelf that keeps "you" chosen. *Because someone wrote your name* breaks on the Postmaster's one breath, *again*. *Because the others burned it* is better, chosen last. *Because you could* breaks on the bench, where the man shows you his January ticket and you do nothing. *Because it would lapse* breaks on *Persons of Note*'s last page, quoted verbatim: *it comes for whoever is here.*

Row 5 is the subversion. The wax never chose anyone, and it will come with his name to the next hand and the next until one finishes it. The act is two sentences, *You sit down beside him. / Later you stand up.*, and no more of it is written. The man keeps his life: always early, the sock, the ticket in his hatband, the number of days to January written in the fog on the lantern glass. The Company's notice lists his own hat as the killer's particular. Row 8 is a but against the Codex: the Hitman does not take the train out but takes the man's place on the bench. The handoff close gives the wax to the hand in front of yours at the window. *It comes for whoever is here. You are here.*
