Wikilink back to [[03_Stories/28_The_Cave_Gate|The Cave Gate]].

The game's V2 Beast and Cave Gate under the wave's lock: the thing is never seen, sized, named or explained. Only its effects reach the page, and each one is a wrongness the Lamplighter must shelve: a lantern dragged inside the bars, a dark stuff that smells of tin and will not mix with water, drag marks in pairs, two knocks, bodies laid down "the way you put down a thing you mean to come back for". Her inner life is her trade (paid by the lantern, docked for a dark one, two touches to each post, the fourth square lantern always last), so the horror attacks her one rule.

The causal ledger has twelve rows. The slip (row 1); but the lantern is inside, black (2); therefore the storm water did it, and she hangs it above the wrack (3); but Kearney is dead on dry shingle where the water never came (4); therefore she takes the town's word, wolves (5); but nine lanterns in the cave and no fur, no claw, and a man taken from the middle of them (6); therefore, more thinly, he stepped out of the others' light and the light kept the eight (7); but the black has come up the shingle to the foot of the two posts lit nearest the water and to no dark door, and the wolves' rule is turned over: it comes to a light (8). Therefore she asks to let the Gate stand dark and is told "lit or docked" (9). But the padlock is turned, not broken, and the Gate stands open on the fourth night (10). Therefore she lights the Gate high to keep it on the shore and leaves the square's fourth lantern dark for the first time (11), and on the 28th she is paid for the one and docked for the other (12).

The ending is complicit: the light is her choice, and someone walks down to it without a lantern while she does not turn. The calendar is shared with *The South-East Light* and *Three Heirlooms*: the hunt at low water on the 23rd, the open Gate on the 27th, the square lit again on the 28th, with nothing at the back of the cave on Christmas night. The close returns to the counting habit: *Seven.*
