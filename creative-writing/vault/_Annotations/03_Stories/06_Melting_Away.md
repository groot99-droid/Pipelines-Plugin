Wikilink back to [[03_Stories/06_Melting_Away|Melting Away]].

A hazing-horror piece structured as a slow surrender of agency: Daniel's decisions get smaller and more coerced at each step (go to the party / take off his glasses / drink the cup / "open up") until the narration itself starts to blur with his intoxication — "Walls dripped like wax. Faces melted into grotesque masks" — a nice technical choice where the prose's own reliability degrades in step with the protagonist's senses rather than staying clinical. The twist is a genre-standard but well-executed victim-to-prey reversal: Matty's "We hate boring" text, repeated verbatim as the opening hook and the closing line, reframes the entire story as one iteration of a repeating predation ritual rather than a one-off tragedy. That repetition is the clearest cyclical-non-resolving ending in this batch — the story doesn't resolve Daniel's fate for the reader so much as it pivots to Matty texting "another name," making clear the horror continues on an open loop with a new victim. This is closer to true-crime horror (a predator working a method, a bystander-officer who shrugs it off as an inevitable statistic) than to the folklore/institutional horror of 07 and 08, but it shares their refusal of a tidy ending.
