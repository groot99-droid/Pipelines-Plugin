Wikilink back to [[03_Stories/10_Tie_by_Tie|Tie by Tie]].

Ensemble-convergence in the mode's classic shape: four `open` threads — Silas Rook's survey, Vane and Tamsin Crumb with the cup, Jory Kitto's tinners, Father Bray's fishers — each given a section and a single point of view, converging on one elemental imbalance: the lake's water sold to the earth. The causal ledger (15 rows, PASS, handoff) has the mode's required structure for a climax: the trestle's swallowing (row 6, a *but*) is the failed first attempt that the going-down (row 11) answers, and the ties the lake took become the ties that lay the last mile — the failure returned as the means. The two vigil beats are the piece's moral hinge: Bray's vigil is a *but* against the sale, and Vane's bread-and-bell is a *but* against the vigil, written so Bray visibly *chooses* rather than being corrupted. The figurative language is water, light and mud throughout ("the lake … so still it seemed to be thinking"; the fen "like a held grudge"), simile-dense as the mode asks, while the codex asides (Thorne's wire, the freight agreement's Clause 4) stay outside the ledger. Restraint: Jory's death is a lamp, a turn and an opening, and he is seen only at rest with his tobacco tin and unfinished spoon; Wenna's arrival on the first train is the handoff, and the closing refrain ("The tie under your boot at dawn is who you are") ties the founding to the game's identity-at-dawn line. Per revision_notes.md, the drafter's italic beat-restating scaffolds were cut and folded into the prose.
