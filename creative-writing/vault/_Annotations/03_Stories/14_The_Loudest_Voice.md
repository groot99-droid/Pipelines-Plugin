Wikilink back to [[03_Stories/14_The_Loudest_Voice|The Loudest Voice]].

Role secrecy kept in prose. The Killer is "you" throughout, and the look is borrowed and never described beyond coat, hat and scarf, so the piece can be read at any seat without naming anyone. The section heads are the Killer card's own clauses in order (*Walk in a borrowed character*, *Strike after dark when the room is empty*, *Clean one clue*, *Wash at the well*, *Then be the loudest voice asking who did it*), with *The town has spoken* and *The basin* added after, so the game's rule text becomes the plot's spine. The causal ledger (11 rows, PASS, consumed) sets the Killer's choices against the Accomplice's help and the Child's counting. The Watchman will stand at her door (*but*): the other one's false cry of wolves draws him off. You believe nobody saw (*but*): a fresh chalk tally on the doorframe, which you wipe because the card allows one clue (*therefore*). At the well a breath on the rungs (*but*) is shelved as mere-rot. At the Bell the thread puts the square's eyes on your scarf (*but*), and the forged dragged-heel trail, the vouch "at the pump, with me" and your call for rope move them to the Watchman's heel (*therefore*). The Watchman is fined and stood down, so nobody stands at any door, and you lay the knife on the sill for the other one. The heir to the knife is prepared and not triggered. The ending is consumed rather than cyclical: the borrowed look will not come off at the basin, and the voice in the throat is the coat's. The repeated first line ("Somebody has to ask first") is texture on top. Restraint: the strike is one sentence and a lantern going out. Hattie's interior life is in the room, not the wound: her name chalked over the copper, the half-written letter to her son, the shawl washed thinner than anything she takes in, and the button jar that *The Undertaker's Register* finds complete. The Child is placed in the crowd counting feet, and the piece never says she made the tally.
