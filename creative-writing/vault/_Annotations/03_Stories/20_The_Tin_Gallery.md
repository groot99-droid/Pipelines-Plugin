Wikilink back to [[03_Stories/20_The_Tin_Gallery|The Tin Gallery]].

The one work in the vault that shows the Beast, and it does so while keeping the Codex's line that nothing more is written. The ledger runs open, then seven therefores and four buts, and its organising fact is in row 2: Norman is under the hill carrying *the only lit lantern*. Every wave-6 piece says creatures keep the edge of the light. This story's buts turn that rule against him. The first but (row 3) is that the light keeps nothing off. Bats come to it and rats run past him, *out of the gallery, not from his light*. Norman's shelves get thinner as they fall. The water coming back is the first (T), and the smear with Reg's unlit pipe in it breaks it (B). *Slipped and crawled down* is the second; Reg found *carried and laid down*, bitten twice and known by his right hand, breaks that (B). The third shelf is the word *wolves* said aloud with no fur, no claw mark and a lantern lit. The boards over the lower drift, lifted, stacked dry and square, with one pair of dry prints in and out and no light carried, break it (row 9), and they are the story's only trace of the Accomplice, a hand that came down without a light.

Row 10 is the hinge, a therefore drawn from two rows (2+9). Because he has the only light and because a hand opened the way down, the Beast comes up to him: big, long and low, slate-grey as wet stone, a mouth and no face. It is shown at Man Eater level and given no name, cause or origin. Two hits kill, as the game has it, so after the first Norman's choice is the light or his life. He sets the lantern turned all the way up on the VANE TIN CO crate beside Reg and climbs out in the dark by Reg's order of the levels. The complicit close gives the Company its words, *lost in the workings*, and keeps the hand's secret; a figure stands at the mouth at first light without a lantern, looks, and goes. The last line is the story's whole verdict: *He came up.*
