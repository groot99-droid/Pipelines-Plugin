Grounds 99 in 2626, reached only through the two lines the vault already holds: the stamp RETAIN UNTIL 2626 (*Subject Files*) and the designation clause THROUGH ANY EXCHANGE, AND AFTER (*Cables*), here copied forward in a registry file nobody can account for. Nothing explains Anomaly Unknown, and nothing new or made walks in the woods: every animal in the Fauna register is ordinary, only bold, and each entry ends on a *Contest:* tag that echoes the Codex Bestiary's "Dread:" without copying it.

The voice is the survey's: third person of himself, past tense, fragment lists, a simile per station at most, drawn from water, wood and frost. The residents go by slot and place, because "the numbers were what got written down, and the numbers were what lasted", which turns wave 4's "you are your number" into an inheritance rather than a sentence. The eight stations follow the game's eight BlackPine scenes under plain names, and what the 1952 works hide stays closed: the plug is not breached, the drowned mouth is not entered, the light under the water is "reported; not verified", and the surveyor watches the ford all night and sees the river.

The two inner lives carry the close. Nought touches the plates 0 to 9 at dusk and passes the empty slot without looking; the girl who came down the grade in a fish basket is entered *over count* and asks whether a number can be taken if nobody has it. The retention rule disposes of the box, burned unread with the stamp going last, but the surveyor refuses the other rule and will not reassign the slot. The girl's 61, chalked *below* the smudge and not touching it, carries the chalked-name thread forward without printing a letter of the name. *Entered as found* is the whole ruling.
