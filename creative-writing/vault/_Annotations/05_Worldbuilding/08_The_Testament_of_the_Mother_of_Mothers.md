Wikilink back to [[05_Worldbuilding/08_The_Testament_of_the_Mother_of_Mothers|The Testament of the Mother of Mothers]].

The Lira Calyx architecture applied to Port-au-Levain: a world-bible opening ("Of the Rock"), Books with run-in subheads, a margin hand that corrects the record from Book One onward, forged blank pages, and a final entry torn mid-sentence that hands the threat to the reader. Redaction carries the politics — the bars always protect the Vane name and never a commoner's, and the goatkeeper Ysolde, the Arch-Fermenter's twice-mended apron and the Black-Crust woman's lost boy are restored by the margin hand, which is how the piece honours the restraint precedent inside an archive designed to erase. The causal ledger (12 rows, PASS, handoff) runs the Vane line: stamped Commis, never to feed the Mother (*therefore* the cup), the short count, verdict in absence; the *but* of tin copper feeding the house he was erased from, the sealing, the *but* of his return, the verdict called due, the *but* of his death before the Hearth, and the Ordinance of Dusk that makes every island night begin with his death — the in-world reason the owner's Port-au-Levain matches open on a death. Row 11 is the payoff the ledger flags as a multi-parent *but*: the barred keyholder of Book Four is the margin hand, Honorine Vane, who confesses by lifting her own bar. The cup "came back lighter than it left", stated twice and never explained, which binds this piece to Tie by Tie's daughter culture without either piece confirming that the rumours ride on it.
