Wikilink back to [[05_Worldbuilding/13_The_Undertaker_s_Register|The Undertaker's Register]].

The forty villager names of `names.json` given forty lives, by the one officer who would have to write every one of them down. The register form carries the reference load (name, trade, habit, burial line) and the plot lives in what the Undertaker does to his own columns. The causal ledger (15 rows, PASS, complicit) breaks his rules one at a time. "I write only what I have carried" is broken by Ivy Cotter and Frank on the first page (*but*). Therefore he walks the living himself and rules a Cause column of his own. Elsie's line dated in his hand breaks "seen with my own eyes" (*but*), and he lays it on the boy, so he locks the book (*therefore*). Mabel's undated line seems to prove the lock (*therefore*). Cyril's date with the key at his neck breaks it (*but*), so he blames his sleeping hand and ropes his wrist (*therefore*). Hattie's date with the knot intact breaks that (*but*), and he leaves his first Cause blank. From there the turns are therefores of surrender: he stops opening (Ada's nailed box, sent unlifted), dates Vera himself to match the bakery, cannot tell which came first, and strikes the Cause heading through. The decay is typographic, as in *Fourteen Things*: a fifth column is ruled, filled, left blank, then struck. The gravestone's ambiguity lock holds by construction. The book never says whether it records or causes, and the last entry is made "before Agnes came down the Row. I think before." Restraint: no death is on the page. Each life is one habit and one kept object (Mabel's hymn-sheet insoles, Cyril's two notes, Hattie's button jar, Rose's geranium carried in at curfew like a child). The jar is the piece's moral centre: the one inventory that comes out complete, and he does not enter it. Three lines stay *neither* (Ivy Cotter, Ada Brook, Frank), and the lead lining "longer than any of his forty" is "the length of a man who is not on my forty", and the register never says whose. Its first lines return as its last.
