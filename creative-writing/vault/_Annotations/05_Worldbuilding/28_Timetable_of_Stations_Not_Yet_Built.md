Wikilink back to [[05_Worldbuilding/28_Timetable_of_Stations_Not_Yet_Built|Timetable of Stations Not Yet Built]].

The wave's futures given the form the world already trusts: the next sheet in *The Daily Train*'s file, the same masthead, columns and Rules of Carriage, with the kept rules quoted verbatim and the rest set as "As 1893." The Company printed it in November and plans as if the Panic will pass. Every future is therefore a halt "under survey, times to be announced", which is exactly as much as an 1894 company could know, and the footnotes do the Codex's refusing ("The Company does not say why"; "The Company does not keep it"; "The ticket reads both. The Company does not say which").

The eras enter physically and stay apart. The second hand writes only what the winter of 1893 knew: the closing wire, "Two out of the cave this month", the Undertaker's ruled-through cause. 1952 arrives as typed slips pasted against the Harrow row, near enough to suggest the old grade and never confirming it, and the 2626 slip is pasted half over them so the earlier type shows at its edge, "the same place, later". The struck "Prop." with its empty blank is the only hint at the Heir, and the 29 December outcome cannot be on a sheet printed in November.

The close is the shape of the whole wave. Rules 13 to 16 extend carriage to stations that do not exist ("A passenger for such a station travels as freight"), the second hand answers "Then who signs for them", and the last printed row is a signal-only stop with no name, holding the bench man's one-way January fare, booked and not punched. The sheet ends on its own footnote: *‡ Name to be announced.*
