The Bible's places and the Tide-Watcher's Lighthouse Sweep ability made into a single form. The ordinance turns the reef lamp landward four times a night, and anyone the beam finds abroad without a seal is logged for the Iron-Wardens. So the gazetteer moves by compass bearing, and every place gets the same three fields: what the beam shows, **Logged**, and **Not logged**. The two fields carry the piece. What Perrine logs gets people taken; what she leaves out is where her conscience lives. The Gallery's cliff houses are dipped "by subscription", and the beam goes dull on the Abbey's walls, so the light itself is classed. The ten rooms of the Sacred Kitchen are entered as ten roof vents, each a game interior seen only as its steam. The Vault is a plume, and the feeding hand has "been a Vane" since before the book, stated as a fact of the office and nothing more. At the Drowners' bearing a logged line is struck through. The Tide-Ghouls are kept at the edge of the beam (kelp trails, a net-mender's knot) so the drowned who walk are heard more than seen. Between the sweeps, close third on Perrine gives her a life: a sou set King-down on the sill, a fourth piece of bread kept in her pocket for someone. The ending is the form going quiet. She logs the ferry and the six bell, and the last **Not logged:** field stays blank. It is a refusal, or a mercy, or a name she will not write even to herself.
