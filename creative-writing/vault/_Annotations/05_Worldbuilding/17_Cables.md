The program's paper trail as plot. Two voices alternate. The cables are capitals, numbered paragraphs and BT/NNNN, and the only feeling in them leaks through their remarks fields ("MASKS FOR LABOUR NOT AUTHORIZED. LUNG-ROT IS A MEASURED VARIABLE."). The duty hand H. keeps the telephone log in plain sentences and a growing conscience. Each agency arrives under its real redacted name and limits its interest in one line: the Bureau to name checks, the Secret Service to the scrip, Counter Intelligence to the loyalty of staff, and the Agency "in support only". In December the four lines come back word for word to the Inspector-General, ending "INSPECTOR-GENERAL IS STAFF". The refrain is the piece's argument: everyone owns a corner and nobody owns the camp. The second refrain is Sawmill No. 3 asking about mail every Friday. His body thins across the log (wrist like a broom handle, ribs like crate slats, breath showing by a lit stove) until "Nobody asked about mail". He comes back once, as a hand on a tray in the cold cabinet, tagged SAWMILL 3 and "entered as a specimen, not as a person". That is the wave's most explicit image, and the owner kept it. The Man Eater seeds sit where the owner placed them: SITE ███, N. DAK. on Ambry's requisitions, "THE REST ONWARD BY SEALED CAR", MAINTAIN BELOW FREEZING, the frost under the searched boards, and the line at 03:10 that stays open and silent. The groan was cut here; only *Fifteen Seconds* sounds it. Continuity locks: "KESS—" is this work's chalked-name fragment; 2626 is a phrase only ("THROUGH ANY EXCHANGE, AND AFTER"); the old grade south of the trestle has "OWNER OF RECORD: NONE"; Moss is "NOT AT WORK FOR REASONS OF RELIGION". The one name H. writes down is Mirek's, "because the rack will not".
