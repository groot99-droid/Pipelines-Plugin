A gazetteer that walks. The fixed entry format (**Light / What stands / What the town says**) keeps the reference value of the World Codex of Wyrmreach, and the Lamplighter's dusk round strings the entries together, so every scene in the game is reached in the order a body would reach it. The **Light** field does double work. Schedule B makes her lanterns the only lawful light after the train goes, so each field also says who is breaking the law: the green lamp in the apothecary loft, the Widow's candle, the Post Office window, the drowned lantern. She reports none of them, and that silence is her character. The **What the town says** field holds the rumour lock: every claim stays "that…", none is ruled, and both readings of the drowned lantern stand side by side. The three rings are the map's Small / Medium / Large profiles turned into in-world boarding ("On nights when there are fewer of us, these doors are boarded"). Habits carry the restraint precedent: the two touches at every post, never humming at the barrel, never looking down the well. The unnamed hanged man is given his whistle and his boot-mender's hands rather than a name the Gazette would not print. The last entry's Light field is left blank, and the piece stops on "She raised the pole." The will's clause ("whoever remains when the last lantern goes out") is left hanging over the reader instead of resolved. Plot logic: not applicable, codex form.
