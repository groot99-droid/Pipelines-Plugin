Wikilink back to [[05_Worldbuilding/11_Mrs_Grieves_s_Tab_Book|Mrs Grieves's Tab Book]].

A codex in the Lira Calyx found-document line, kept as a bar ledger. Every page head is the same triplet: **Rate** (the falling scrip, 3 to 5 to the dollar), **Low water** (the island's tide, circled), **Stone** (the gravestone's name, or "Did not go up"). The town's three clocks are made visible on one line, and the habit of not looking builds to the last page's "I have not been to look." The House Rules are written against the Articles of the Line. Rule 4 ("A closed tab stays closed… A tab is not an account") is her quiet refusal of Article XI's "the Account Survives". That refusal is the document's politics: she can close a dead person's debt where the Company will not, and does, for Maud, "Square." The dead are kept as habits on a line rather than as bodies: Wilf's mouth-organ in G, Dora Finch's sums upside down, Stan Pike paying all on the night his name was on the stone, Maud paying in darning. The barman's line, one Liquid Courage carried to the top of every page, makes the drowned lantern's other reading into an open account. The book ends on a new line, name to follow, as the barman's last pour comes round again. Bar is written two ways (Frank thrown out; the strike round, the Burial Club, the shells), and she has "written both down". Locks held: Bray "Died or ran"; the drummer's mirroring disbelieved and written anyway; "I do not ask who." Plot logic: not applicable, codex form.
