Wikilink back to [[05_Worldbuilding/25_The_South_East_Light|The South-East Light]].

The V1.5 "lighthouse" made to obey the Ledger. Entry XII rules that there is no lighthouse at HollowMere and Entry XI that there is no sea, so the owner's ruling turns the game's three-storey light into a second marsh light: a Company lamp on an iron post opposite Fennick's, first lit on 18 December, after the Field Guide's last page. The keeper says it himself in the head note ("The Company calls mine a light ... the town will call it Fennick's other one"), and *sea door* survives only as Tuck's word, corrected on the page.

The engine is the signalman's rule, "a man who logs what he did not see is how two trains meet." Every entry is a list of lights, and the reader, holding the other HollowMere works, knows what each light is while the keeper never does: the platform post first, the square's fourth lantern always last after "the wait", the Gate lantern at the boards (the Lamplighter's seventh post in *The Cave Gate*), the cottage window where Nora's girl reads her tide book. Restraint is the form itself. Two deaths happen at the boards and he logs only that "fewer went back up the shingle than came down"; the creature never enters his columns.

The printed form does the uncanny work quietly: its "Boards clear" box appears only on the second and fourth night of every page, which is the game's Nights 2 and 4, and a printer back east is never wrong about a mere he has never seen. Rule 1 (Day and Dusk kept by the Train) turns on him when the line shuts; he writes *Against Rule 1* in full rather than burn to empty, and *By the Train* on the 29th beside the book's only question mark. The 30 December pillar gets *One* in the column and nothing in the Remarks, which keeps the Holy War open. The close leaves *Extinguished at:* blank, the log still on the post, Rule 2 never met.
