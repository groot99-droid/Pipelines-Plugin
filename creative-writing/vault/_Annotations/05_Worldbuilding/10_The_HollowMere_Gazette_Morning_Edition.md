Wikilink back to [[05_Worldbuilding/10_The_HollowMere_Gazette_Morning_Edition|The HollowMere Gazette, Morning Edition]].

Paperwork as horror, as in its sibling the Daily Train. The printed columns are flat Company copy. The plot lives in a compositor's time-stamped pencil (9.40 to 3.55), and Comp. 7 is never named. The causal ledger (11 rows, PASS, cyclical) moves column to column. "A common name" is broken by the rent list's "third door" (*but*). The wire's answer leads him to strike the name (*therefore*). The coffin's blank booking breaks "only the Company's blank" (*but*). The paid-ahead wake suggests "the cough" (*therefore*). The grey thread at the Bandit's till breaks that comfort (*but*). The Gazette's own standing rule (no name unverified; "the Town may supply it") does the turning. Every attempt to protect Ada by leaving a blank turns out to hand the blank to the town, so he restores her name: "Better mine than theirs." The pencil grows shorter as his agency drains, and the last two columns carry none. The standing rule opens and closes the page word for word. Restraint holds through objects. Ada exists through a darned scarf that was her mother's, a reward offered in bread "until Christmas", a wake she paid ahead "so the Row not be charged", and the fact that she asked twice. Locks: the stone "does not account for it"; whether Ada is the Bandit is never settled; Bray's fate is "none answered"; the Fenwood light is "The Company has not lit it". The red darn on her scarf deliberately echoes Father Bray's glove in *What the Well Remembers*, and the owner chose to keep the echo.
