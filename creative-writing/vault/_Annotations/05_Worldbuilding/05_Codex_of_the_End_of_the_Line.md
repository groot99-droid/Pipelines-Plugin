Wikilink back to [[05_Worldbuilding/05_Codex_of_the_End_of_the_Line|Codex of the End of the Line]].

A reference codex in the World Codex of Wyrmreach's architecture — bare headings, fixed-format entries, compressed run-on lists, one-line laws, history told twice (premise paragraph, then a numbered timeline of noun phrases) — but voiced by the game's own world-weary narrator, whose habit of threes becomes the codex's organising principle: three towns, three clocks ("Train. Ferry. Whistle."), three ladders of law with three verdicts each ("One of them is always rope, fire or blade."). The Bible's isomorphic design spine is turned from a developer table into a cosmology: the four vendettas become "the four ways a town breaks" (faith, the Company, the till, a person), and the cross-map mirroring is written as Law 2, stated and never explained. The Cosmic Origin section frames its four elements as belief, not fact ("Not a god. Not a law. A timetable."), which is the codex's main ambiguity device; Law 4 ("Rumour crosses water. Whether it walks, swims or is carried, no one has seen.") holds the owner's rumour lock. The Rest of the Town gives each role card a town nickname and a "stands / knows" line, with the card's own text as the tag — the nicknames are flagged inventions in revision_notes.md. The timeline was revised to carry the wave's founding canon (the lake let down into Vane's gallery, Jory Kitto, the Company bell, Vane's island death) so the codex agrees with its sibling pieces.
