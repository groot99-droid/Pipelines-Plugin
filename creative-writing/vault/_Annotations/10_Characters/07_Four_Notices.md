Wikilink back to [[10_Characters/07_Four_Notices|Four Notices]].

The four vendettas of `vendettas.json`, told as the game would post them. Each notice is a diptych. The **front** is Company print: deed, the three particulars (Height, Scarf, Hat, the game's forensic traits), trace, reward in falling scrip, and the same blank name line, "Unverified. The Town may supply it." The **back** is a hand in pencil or ink from someone the vendetta has used. The fronts carry the mechanics as law. The Zealot's lists the Dark Positions as persons sought and notes "The High Priest. NOT SOUGHT". The Activist's carries the Notice of Redemption, and the Bandit's numbers its four scores, two of them "Not yet". The Hitman's prints the black-wax exemption under Article VI. The backs carry what the fronts cannot price. The candle-keeper counts four proofs that the robes are not her Vicar ("I am still his. I no longer know which of them that makes me"). The railyard sleeper reads the reward in weeks and sees the countersign below the stamp, "a hand I have seen before". Tamsin has three gold coins and two bootprints that disagree, one long and one short, and she fits both. The Hitman's refuser burned the letter and kept her life the size it was, while somebody else's hand did not. Owner rulings are kept as hints. The Baron is assassinated on the platform on 14 December, the reward is "by order of the Heir to the Baron", and "They say Thorne had no sons. They say it the way people say a thing they have been told to say." All four killers stay unnamed, and each back ends on a tag line that states the vendetta's theme. The frame closes on the board turned at dawn, four hands where the print was, and the game's reveal line bent to the blank: "The blank on the board at dawn is who you are." Continuity: the five marked buildings match *The Holy War*, and the chapel plate is taken on 10 December with a .45 casing standing upright and no shot heard.
