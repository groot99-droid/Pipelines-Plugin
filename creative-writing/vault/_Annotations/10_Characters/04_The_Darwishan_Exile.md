Wikilink back to [[10_Characters/04_The_Darwishan_Exile|The Darwishan Exile]].

Modeled on Mahmoud Darwish, this variant is built from land-as-memory imagery — olive trees, soil, homeland as "not a suitcase" — and its strongest lines carry real political-poetic weight ("Exile is more than a geographical concept. You can be an exile in your homeland, in your own house, in a room"). What makes this profile distinct from the other three is also its weakest seam: roughly the back third of its "Sayings" list drifts from exile/homeland material into generic romantic-love couplets ("You are the moon to my ocean, the sun to my sky") that read as stock love-poem filler rather than Darwish's actual register, making this the most tonally uneven of the four profiles — worth a craft note if these profiles get revised, since the other three (Bottonian, Kafkan, Rossettian) stay consistently in their modeled voice throughout. No tie to any specific existing novel, story, or world in this vault was found; like its siblings, it reads as a standalone wandering-mystic archetype entry rather than a character already placed in one of the archive's narratives.
