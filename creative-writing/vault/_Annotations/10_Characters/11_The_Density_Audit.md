Wikilink back to [[10_Characters/11_The_Density_Audit|The Density Audit]].

The island's answer to *The Tally Rack*: the same form, a register of ten weighed identities, with the class line drawn through the scale itself. The ten platters are the island's offices, as cards are on the other maps. Le Levain (the house of Vane), the High Chancellor, the five guild roles of the Bible with their two evolutions left as the office's possible futures, the Inquisitor-in-Training, the Guild Bailiff and the Plongeur. Each is entered with seal, tariff and one habit or kept object, so every subject has a life. The Standing Rule supplies the plot. The Gallery's bread is *declared* and never put on the balance, and the Abbey's is "weighed by God". So when the rack comes out four onces over, the Proof-Reader can weigh only the poor. The four onces are the weight of the seal, and they turn up in the one loaf with no thumb-press in it, Petit-Yann's. He presses every loaf he owns, and his quarterly question about his own loaf's weight is entered as a habit. The register never says he was framed. It enters the arithmetic again with the seal taken out, notes that no one has reported anything missing, and records a request that the declared bread be weighed, which comes back down from the gallery under seal. It stops at eleven in the morning with the fine unpaid. *Shanty for the Morning Ferry* has the noon bell and the Hearth. Kept at the edge: two of the Hearth-Warden's apprentices are shackled for singing, and the Proof-Reader did not write down what they sang.
