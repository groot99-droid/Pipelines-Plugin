Wikilink back to [[10_Characters/01_Graknox_the_Ironbound_Raider|Graknox the Ironbound Raider]].

This profile builds its archetype through accretion rather than scene: Origins, Appearance, Personality, Combat Style, Philosophy, and a bank of "Raider Sayings" each add one more brushstroke to a stock-but-effective northern-barbarian silhouette, closing with an explicit "Potential Story Hooks" list that marks this as a reusable character-bank asset rather than a character already embedded in a plot. The raider sayings ("Fear is a guest — invite it in, learn its name, then send it on its way") do the heaviest characterization work, establishing a culture of disciplined stoicism through proverb rather than through the Combat Style section's more generic "brute force and calculated precision" language. Worth flagging for cross-project tracking: in the source master document this file sits immediately before the World Codex of Wyrmreach (and just before the WrymWretch novel plot), but nothing in Graknox's own text references dragons, elves, twin-souls, or Wyrmreach at all — no shared vocabulary, no shared geography, no shared magic system. Positional adjacency in the source file is not, on this evidence, the same as narrative membership; he reads as a self-contained sword-and-sorcery archetype rather than a Wyrmreach character, and no `project` tag has been assigned on that basis.
