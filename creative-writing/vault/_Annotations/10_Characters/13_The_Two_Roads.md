Wikilink back to [[10_Characters/13_The_Two_Roads|The Two Roads]].

Book V's story-mode arcs (every keeper on three maps with a default and two paths) made into a deck rather than a fate. Each card has the same layout (Face, One way, The other way, Laid for, How it lies), so the reader learns it once. Each road is conditional ("if gold comes as a tip ..."), and a road whose trigger has already fired in canon is read as open, not taken: Tamsin's card "would not lie still" because both of its triggers have fired. The three suits are in time order, the island, then HollowMere, then Grounds 99, and nothing on the page says one deck travelled.

The readers carry the restraint of each map. The island card-woman will not read the Gallery ("it decrees") and lays no card for the platter "for a name under seal". Wenna, her chalk pawned since 4 December, reads only for the living with the Lantern's playing cards. Slot 0 enters his reading as a supplementary sheet, "not for the Tribunal". The kept details are small and specific: the apprentice who eats the scale's crumbs, the yellow palms the Wharf takes for a thief's, the Postmaster asking to be read to for the first time since he was small, the Clerk in the Lantern door saying "Miss Cotter paid the wick". The Heir stays a hint: the man to be guarded is not on the card, and the guards are, seven of them.

The close is a missing card. Danny Fitch's deck has no queen of hearts, so the Tallyman's own colour is the card that is not there, and the five *Marked: ______* lines go back into the register blank, as *The Tally Rack* has them on 20 December. The last line inverts the yard's refrain (the log at dawn is who you are) without quoting it: *It is only which way you were facing.*
