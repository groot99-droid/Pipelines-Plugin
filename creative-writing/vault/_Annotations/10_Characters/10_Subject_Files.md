The wave's bestiary as the program would keep it. Each of the four bosses (the owner's four hidden Mob Boss Fights) gets the same file: former name redacted, billet, trial letter with a euphemism in quotation marks, associates, housing as assigned (the game's four hideouts: the hollow pine, the flooded stope, the Bunkhouse root cellar, the mess hall after roll), condition at last observation, property envelope, one verbatim statement, observation, and the same verdict, RESULT: anomalous. CAUSE: unknown. The repetition is the cruelty. The conditions are at the owner's Man Eater level (a jaw pushed forward two fingers' width, lengthened fingers wrinkled to the shoulder, lids drawn back to the brow at 96 lb from 171), and each is answered by an envelope that holds the person: spectacles and a seed catalogue with apple varieties ticked twice, a thimble and a hair ribbon, a sealed letter *To be read when I sleep*. The statements are the files' heart: "Leave the window." "Has a letter come." "Count me." The Choir's statement is "Not taken individually", and its readiness table ends 12/12/12 after one voice behind the beat is "corrected". P-0 is the abduction mechanic written as a found extract (gather, sedate with the line withheld, carry using the roll; three trials, two failures and the subject is closed). It gives no technique and breaks off mid-sentence at "On compromise of the facility —". SF-5 makes the owner's ruling a file: one scientist taken at the sealed car, a search by the hour, then "Facility: not found. Subjects carried: not found. Remains: none", and the pencil hand's "almost like they had help". The N. Dak. transfer line and the 2626 retention stamp are the two Man Eater and future seeds. The Windigo is never named. The last page is an empty form, and the gate's catechism changes one word: "The number on the file at dawn is who you are."
