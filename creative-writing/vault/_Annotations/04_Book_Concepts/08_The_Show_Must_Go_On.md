Wikilink back to [[04_Book_Concepts/08_The_Show_Must_Go_On|The Show Must Go On]].

The hook is a head injury that doesn't damage the protagonist physically so much as re-genres his perception — his life starts reading to him as a multi-camera sitcom, complete with canned-laughter jokes only he hears and friends he addresses by surname "as if it's a TV show." That's a lighter, tonally different kind of unreliable-narrator horror than the rest of the folder: instead of dread building toward a threat, the wrongness is normalized almost immediately ("before long it just becomes normal for him"), which trades suspense for a quieter, sadder note about a person losing his grip on consensus reality without anyone — including him — treating it as an emergency. This is also the most unfinished entry in the folder, ending mid-thought on "Work in progress…" rather than a pitch-style closing thesis, which marks it as a rawer fragment than the polished ad-copy pitches (Peephole, Campsites, Beetles, Alarm Clock) sitting alongside it.
