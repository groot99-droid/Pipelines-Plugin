Wikilink back to [[04_Book_Concepts/05_Chasing_Cold_Clues|Chasing Cold Clues]].

This is the outlier of the folder: not horror at all, but a pulp-adventure series pitch in the Indiana Jones mold, built from a character dossier (bio first, plot second) rather than a premise-first hook — a structurally different pitch-doc template than the other seven files use. The three-beat serial shape (chase → cryptic clue → jungle ultimatum) is compressed almost to a treatment or back-of-book-jacket level, and it ends on an explicit "To be continued...", marking this as the opening installment of an ongoing series rather than a self-contained novel concept. The prose leans on stock adventure-genre phrasing ("seasoned adventurer and scholar," "powerful adversaries who will stop at nothing") more than the other concepts, which read more like original dread-building; this one reads more like a franchise pitch than an atmosphere piece, which is worth keeping in mind if it's ever expanded — the voice will need more specificity to stand apart from genre convention.
