Wikilink back to [[04_Book_Concepts/07_Narrative_Voice|Narrative Voice]].

Despite sitting in the "book concepts" folder under a title about voice, this is a reincarnation/soul-cycle concept: a godlike consciousness watches and subtly edits its own past self, each life ending in the same collapse-then-recovery pattern before the whole cycle repeats, and the book itself is structured to repeat that cycle at the macro level, closing on an explicit pivot to "the concept of reincarnation (give multiple views, and ideology on this)." That is close enough in premise — one consciousness threading itself across successive, self-editing lifetimes toward eventual peace — to warrant flagging a likely connection to [[02_Novels/04_The_Cycle_of_Us]], which runs the same nine-lives soul-lineage structure in far more developed form; this file may be an early, compressed seed of that later piece rather than an unrelated concept. Structurally it's also the densest, least-broken-out pitch in the folder — a single unbroken paragraph doing the work of theme statement, plot mechanism, and structural instruction all at once, in contrast to the bulleted, white-space-heavy horror pitches elsewhere in this folder.
