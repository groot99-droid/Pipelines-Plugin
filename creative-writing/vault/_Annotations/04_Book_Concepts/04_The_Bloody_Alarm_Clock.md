Wikilink back to [[04_Book_Concepts/04_The_Bloody_Alarm_Clock|The Bloody Alarm Clock]].

Classic cursed-object structure: an inherited artifact that cannot be destroyed or discarded, escalating harm that blurs the line between dream and physical injury, and a closing beat that resets the curse onto a new owner rather than resolving it — the same self-perpetuating-object shape as folklore chain-curses (chain letters, monkey's paws). The bulleted symptom list (waking soaked, feeling faint, phantom ticking) is this folder's now-familiar escalation-by-inventory move, here used to make the dream/reality bleed concrete rather than abstract. The reveal line "The clock doesn't measure time. It consumes it." is doing the pitch's thesis-statement work in miniature — a one-line hook built to be pull-quoted, which is consistent with how these pitches keep landing their premise in an aphorism rather than a scene. Worth noting the victim is never named or given interiority beyond fear and exhaustion; like Missing Campsites, the piece is building dread from situation and object rather than character.
