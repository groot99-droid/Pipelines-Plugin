Wikilink back to [[06_Songs/11_Six_Things_a_Hand_Can_Make|Six Things a Hand Can Make]].

The crafting system as a smith's work song, sung over a hammer count. The six materials and six recipes are the game's, and the song keeps its rule that things are made only in lit, watched rooms (*Nothing gets made in the dark in this camp*). The making itself is never shown. Each verse gives a material's provenance and its cost: the saw that has *run quiet since Monday*; stone, *the only thing they carry me that nobody died for*; resin, which the Reverend thumbed on brows at the gate before he was gone on the fourth, while the collar still blesses at the same height of hand. That last line folds in *Resin-Seal* and the Tunnel and searchlight dread of Grounds 99 without naming a boss. The mushrooms are the one thing she cannot make into anything, *Thank God for one thing in this camp that isn't mine to make*.

The refrain does the song's moral work. *Bring it to the forge, I'll make it. / Don't tell me who it's for* is a smith's professional innocence, and the song wears it away until it reads *Somebody tell me who I was for*. Carl, her brother, is the name on the list, and the song never says which tool reached him. Ada's name echoes Ada Brook in HollowMere, as the wave-4 ruling allows, and nothing in the song names HollowMere. The last line, *The seventh thing a hand can make is a name, and nothing in this forge can unmake it*, adds the one recipe the game does not have.
