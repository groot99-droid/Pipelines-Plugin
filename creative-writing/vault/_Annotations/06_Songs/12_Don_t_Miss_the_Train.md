Wikilink back to [[06_Songs/12_Don_t_Miss_the_Train|Don't Miss the Train]].

The companion the *Ballad of the Iron Beast* outline flagged and never wrote, sung by the same platform hand on the last platform of the year. It keeps that song's machinery: a time-stamped cold open, crew roll-calls, a spoken break in capitals, stage directions and a half-spoken outro. Its hooks are deliberate callbacks: *smoke hangin' like chain*, *she don't wait, don't explain*, *Freight is freight, and the Company don't ask, so don't I*. The refrain carries the turn over four returns, from the opening narration's creed (*and whatever you do, boys — don't miss the train*), through *don't miss the last train* and *she won't come back for you*, to the confession: *Nobody missed the last train, boys — we just stood where it left us. Again.*

The bridge is the mode's turn on a single *But I had a ticket.* He finally takes Arthur's ticket out of the hatband, never punched past Tuesday, and gives it to Ernie, the twelve-year-old water boy who asks every train where she goes after us. Ernie repeats the Iron Beast's lines exactly, for a new boy: *He looked at me once. He stepped back down. / He stayed for me.* The singer's confession is a triad that ends on what it costs him: *I took it back. I put it in the band. I was glad.* The town is seen from the boards in one line each: Nora's lantern on the dock, the long coats who stayed, the empty bench of the twelfth, and whoever did for the Baron waving the train off. The heavy case is handed up without a face. A woman and a boy run down the boards after the train in the outro. The closer states the cycle outright: *we just hand the ticket to the next boy, and watch him give it back.*
