The five townsfolk trades and their two evolutions each (GDD §3), written as the letter each writer composes on the night of the fork. Every letter goes to someone who will never read it: the dead Vane, Hester who cannot read, the vanished Bray, the drowned barman, Ivy Cotter "east or the back car". That absence is the licence to confess. The epigraph from Article VI ("The stamp is not evidence of reading") sets the terms for a town where every letter is steamed. Each letter keeps its fork visible without ruling it. Tamsin's "I could bake to his list… I could take what comes to the back step" holds Ration Tyrant and Fence in one sentence, and she has "decided" without saying which. The Postmaster's first rung, a man buying a stamp he did not need, is extortion as a glance. The Undertaker weighs Chief Coroner against what lead is for ("Lead keeps a thing where you put it"). The Bartender's fork is the formal stroke. The letter is written twice, identical in its frame and divergent in its middle (the strike round and the Burial Club against the tooth under the rail and two shells off the train), and one copy goes in the stove, so the reader never learns which. The Clerk's letter, dated 15 December after the owner's assassination ruling, carries both of his evolutions at once. The gap between the scrip posted at morning and counted at noon is the Embezzler. The writ raising him to Heir to the Baron is the other, a man his mother "never said his name either", and a writ that "names the town where she kept rooms", a town he never put on any Company form. The bastard-son reading stays a hint, as approved. Every letter ends on an aphoristic line in the confessional mode, and the last bends the game's dawn line: "The name you sign at midnight is who you are." Ivy Cotter's three readings (Settled, Gone east, the thimble box) are kept as the owner chose. Plot logic: not applicable, letter form.
