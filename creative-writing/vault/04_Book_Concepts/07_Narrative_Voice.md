Narrative Voice

A god overlooks himself in the past as he makes changes to produce his favored outcome but in the end nothing changes. Throughout the book a person goes through life with the narrative of his conscious voice remaining stationery, watching himself from an outward view. Over each moment in his life he becomes slightly more conscious as his realization of be a god comes into view which also correspond to characters years of life which he created each with just minor tweaks in his behavior that lead to a all of a sudden change where he is completely different followed by many days depressed thinking about life and ultimately leading to bad times in his life before he finally manages to get a hold of it then the cycle repeats. Later on towards the end of life the book repeats starting back with the god but this time the concept of reincarnation (give multiple views, and ideology on this).
