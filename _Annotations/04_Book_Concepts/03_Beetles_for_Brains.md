Wikilink back to [[04_Book_Concepts/03_Beetles_for_Brains|Beetles for Brains]].

This concept runs on institutional-gaslighting horror layered over body horror: the boot camp insists the sleep schedule is normal while a catalogued list of symptoms (disorientation, memory slippage, clicking sounds, phantom sensations) accumulates in "some recruits... some recruits..." parallel structure, the same escalation-by-inventory device used in Behind the Peephole and Missing Campsites but pointed inward at the body instead of outward at environment or strangers. The pitch keeps deliberate ambiguity about whether "the bugs" are literal parasites, a myth, or a psychological-ops tactic — that unresolved triple reading is the actual hook, and collapsing it too early would flatten the premise. Sleep-as-vulnerability is the clearest theme statement in the folder ("the most vulnerable moment in your life... might be the moment something else takes hold"), stated almost as bluntly as ad copy, which is consistent with how thesis-forward these pitch docs are across the board.
