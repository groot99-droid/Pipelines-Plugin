Wikilink back to [[04_Book_Concepts/02_Missing_Campsites|Missing Campsites]].

The structural engine is a countdown told through erosion rather than attack — each day removes a fixed point from the world (campsites, then signage, then the sky itself) instead of introducing a threat, so dread accumulates through subtraction. This is the clearest "unnoticed disappearance" premise in the folder: the horror isn't that something is coming for the campers, it's that everyone else's indifference proves reality itself is negotiable and they are the only anchors left in it. The pacing compresses an entire cosmic-horror arc into three numbered days (arrival, first vanishings, full erasure), which is typical of these outlines but especially tight here — there's no room shown for character interiority, only escalating environmental wrongness. One concrete beat worth noting: the reveal that phones show "no signal, no time, no date" is doing double duty as both a horror image and a quiet implication that time itself, not just geography, has stopped applying to them.
