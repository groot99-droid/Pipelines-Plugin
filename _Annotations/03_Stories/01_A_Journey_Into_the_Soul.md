Wikilink back to [[03_Stories/01_A_Journey_Into_the_Soul|A Journey Into the Soul]].

The whole piece runs on a single sustained conceit — the eye as a planet — and refuses to break it even once ("This is not metaphor. / This is fieldwork."), which is what keeps a potentially twee premise reading as clinical and eerie instead of cute. Structurally it's a descent narrative told as a gazetteer: five discrete biomes (desert, sea, forest, pupil-abyss, soullight), each introduced with a bolded emoji-header and built from short, declarative sentence fragments stacked in triplets ("Dust storms rise without warning. / The terrain feels ancient, eroded by time and blinking."). There's no human protagonist — the pov is carried by "light" itself as a non-character observer, which lets the prose stay in a floating present tense throughout. Unlike this vault's horror pieces, the ending here is not cyclical or withheld — it resolves upward into direct thematic statement ("Every eye contains a universe. / Every universe contains a soul."), a tidy, almost sermonic close rather than an unresolved loop. Worth noting as a structural sibling to 02 (Heartstream): both use anatomy-as-cosmology and a journey-through-the-body frame, but this one ends in recognition/wonder while 02 ends in loss.
