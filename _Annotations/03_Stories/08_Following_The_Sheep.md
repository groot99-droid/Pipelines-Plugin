Wikilink back to [[03_Stories/08_Following_The_Sheep|Following The Sheep]].

Runs the same catalogued-incident escalation structure as 07, but through a corporate-interview frame instead of a military-base frame: numerology (66 floors, 666 offices), an occult logo, an invasive personal-history form, a blood draw, a self-operating elevator, a self-navigating hallway — each anomaly noted, flinched at, then rationalized away by Shaun ("It's just a crazy coincidence") before the next one arrives, building dread by accretion of detail rather than through a single scene of danger. The prose is careful to degrade Shaun's agency in stages that mirror the plot's escalation — first he's merely uncomfortable, then his "motor skills were no longer under his control," then he becomes "a mere passenger in his own body" — so the loss-of-control theme is tracked at the sentence level, not just the plot level, culminating in him signing the contract in his own blood while explicitly unable to stop his hand. The ending refuses resolution or rescue: Shaun becomes "a servant in the dark lord's empire," a transformation-into-complicity close structurally identical to 07's Fisk-becomes-the-monster turn — both protagonists end the story as the thing that consumed them, not freed from it, and neither story circles back to undo the harm.
