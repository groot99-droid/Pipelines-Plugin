Wikilink back to [[03_Stories/07_Project_-_Man_Eater|Project: Man Eater]].

This is the clearest instance in the 03_Stories batch of the vault-wide escalation-by-catalogued-incident tic: the story doesn't build dread through a single rising action so much as through a checklist of discrete, itemized wrongnesses that Fisk notices and mentally files past — the base is armored to keep things in, not out; the crew "look sick and slightly anorexic"; there are claw marks on the walls; it's colder inside than outside; three numbered rules are handed down by Sam; the bunkmate vanishes at exactly 10:00; the meal makes him hungrier the more he eats; his body starts visibly thinning. Each item is delivered almost as a bullet point noticed-and-dismissed ("These things had unsettled Fisk, but he had been in worse places") before the next one stacks on top, which is exactly the catalogued-incident escalation pattern rather than scene-by-scene dramatized tension — the prose tells us the inventory of wrongness more than it stages it. The ending is a full twist-victim-becomes-monster: Fisk isn't rescued or does not escape, he is disassembled as a test subject and explicitly compared to "an animal in a zoo," an unresolved, non-cyclical, bleakly final close (flagged with a blunt "The end.") that denies catharsis rather than looping back like 06's predator does. Institutionally, Sam and the CIA framework echo 08's corporate/occult authority figure — both stories run a bureaucratic middle-manager who calmly administers a cosmic horror on behalf of unseen "superiors."
