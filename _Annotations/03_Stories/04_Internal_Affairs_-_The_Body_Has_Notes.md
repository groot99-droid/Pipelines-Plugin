Wikilink back to [[03_Stories/04_Internal_Affairs_-_The_Body_Has_Notes|Internal Affairs: The Body Has Notes]].

Twelve near-identical sketches built on one comic engine: a body part gets a distinct voice and petty grievance, and the brain is cast as the put-upon moderator ("Brain: / 'Everyone shut up. We're going to smile politely and not embarrass ourselves.'"). The joke format is consistent enough to read like a bit repeated with variations — city walk, crush, road rage, diet, gym, date, interview, horror movie, snack aisle, argument, bed — each one escalating a mundane trigger into a mock-parliamentary floor debate among organs. Craft-wise the funniest choice is giving the "silent" or involuntary body parts (armpits, wallet, willpower) the most self-aware, defeated dialogue, which is where the comedic timing actually lands rather than the jaw/heart/stomach bits that repeat almost verbatim across scenes (the jaw's "Say the word" line appears twice nearly unchanged, in scenes 3 and 11 — a reused beat worth flagging for revision). The cycle ends, appropriately, without resolution: scene 12 closes on "His body: 'We refuse,'" a cyclical non-resolving ending that mirrors the whole piece's structure — no scene actually resolves the internal argument, they just cut away, which is the right choice for a comedic sketch cycle but is the same shape (unresolved loop) this vault's horror pieces use for dread rather than punchline.
