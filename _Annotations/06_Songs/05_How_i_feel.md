Wikilink back to [[06_Songs/05_How_i_feel|How i feel]].

The lyric works in loose, near-rhyming couplets that prioritize internal slant rhyme over strict meter ("Feels like friends have left me all alone / Walking corpses, not a word just a groan"), a looseness that suits its subject: isolation and creative burnout rather than polished self-assurance. The second verse's swing into industry critique ("Everyones hungry for new ideas... Burning us out before we first sprout") reframes the isolation as generational and systemic rather than purely personal, and the piece pivots on direct rhetorical questions — "When did it become overrated to be outrageous, am I outdated?" — that function as the emotional hinge between complaint and reflection rather than being answered outright. The recurring anxiety about wasted time ("Every day I sit and rest / Is another day I can't invest") gives the song a ticking, mortality-adjacent undercurrent that pays off in the final stanza's turn to an afterlife image — "When we all head to heaven... we'll see we were just living free" — which resolves the song's restlessness not by solving it but by relocating the payoff to some later, undefined vantage point, an ending that reads as more wishful than certain.
