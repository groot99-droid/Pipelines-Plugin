Wikilink back to [[07_Poems_and_Prose/18_My_Wish|My Wish]].

Built from four quatrains, the poem works almost entirely by negation before arriving at its affirmation: three stanzas open "My wish is not for..." (material worries, aging, unconditional love, wealth) and only the third stanza turns to state the actual wish — "No my wish is to be me. / Not better or worse. / Not to live with remorse." That plainness is the poem's real subject: it wants nothing external, only permission to exist as-is. The final line breaks the poem's otherwise controlled, almost prayer-like cadence with a deliberate register shift into profanity — "My wish is to be, who ever the fuck that my be" — using the crudeness (and the ungrammatical "my be" rather than "may be") as a closing act of defiance against the polish the rest of the poem maintains.
