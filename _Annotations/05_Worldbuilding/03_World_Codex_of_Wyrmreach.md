Wikilink back to [[05_Worldbuilding/03_World_Codex_of_Wyrmreach|World Codex of Wyrmreach]]. This is the setting bible for the novel [[02_Novels/02_WrymWretch|WrymWretch]] — "Wyrmreach" is the in-world place name the novel's prose refers to (spelled "#Wrymreach" there), and the codex supplies the cosmology the novel's prologue dramatizes scene by scene.

Structurally this is a pure reference codex, not narrative prose — headers for cosmic origin, elven races, draconian races, geography, bestiary, magic system, factions, and timeline, each rendered as compact catalogue entries rather than scenes. Its organizing device is a rigid six-fold symmetry: six elf tribes (Wood/Sky/Earth/Water/Dark/Celestial), each bonded to exactly one twin dragon, each with its own faction, deity-type, and color/biome palette — a taxonomy built for easy expansion (new tribe, new dragon, new realm slots in without disturbing the others). The magic system is memory-based rather than the more common mana or elemental-cost systems: reality itself is sustained by memory, so the central threat (Shallock Izladar's remnants erasing memory to force his return) is a metaphysical erasure-of-history threat rather than a physical invasion, which is what lets the novel's opening chapters treat "the forest is forgetting" as an active horror beat rather than a static worldbuilding fact. The Threefold Bond (Symbiotic Royal Bond / Ancestral Hybridization / Twin-Soul Incarnation) is stated but never dramatized here — it's left for the novel to unpack, which is exactly the kind of load-bearing gap a codex is supposed to hand off to prose.
